heterostructure inhibits the formation of voids when the devices
are under operational conditions. Therefore, the champion
device based on 1,2-DITFB exhibited a PCE of 22.59% and
the corresponding unencapsulated device maintained 96.15%
of their initial PCE after 500 h continuous operation aging under
the illumination. This study provides a new way to regulate the
strength of halogen bonding between perovskite and molecules
via bidentate mode.
