Optoelectronic optimization indicates that piecewise-homogeneous bandgap grading outperforms both
linear and nonlinear bandgap grading. Experimental studies have demonstrated the practical viability of
implementing such grading, with successful demonstrations of front-junction grading [8, 10–12]. However,
challenges remain in maintaining crystalline quality and minimizing defect formation across graded interfaces.
Continued development of advanced fabrication methods and interface-engineering strategies will be key to
fully realizing the beneﬁts of bandgap grading in high-efﬁciency CdTeSe devices.
