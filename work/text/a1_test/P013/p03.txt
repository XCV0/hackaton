As shown by its cross‐sectional SEM images in Figure 1,
the horizontal size of CZTGS‐modified CZTS grains could
be enlarged to ~2 μm when the Li/(Liþ Cu) ratio was
0.07 and further enlarged to ~3 μm with the increasing
Li/(Liþ Cu) ratio to 0.15, greatly reducing the number of
detrimental grain boundaries. The universally enlarged
grain size is also proved by low‐magnification images in
Figure S1. However, a higher value at 0.24 tended to limit
the benefit of grain enlargement with the generation of
the detrimental near‐horizontal grain boundaries. The
reasons for the decreased grain size with more Li addition
will be discussed later. Interestingly, we could identify
liquid‐like layers at the CZTS/MoS2 interface for the Li‐
added sample other than the CZTGS‐modified samples
(Figure S1). This indicates that the Li addition did not
change the LGG grain growth mechanism with a liquid‐
like CTS bottom layer, as was described in our previous
work.17
