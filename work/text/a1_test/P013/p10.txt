impedance analyzer (Keithley E4990A) at an AC bias of
50 mV (10 kHz) with a DC bias voltage sweeping from
−1.6 to 0.6 V. DLCP measurement was performed with
the ac amplitude varied from 10 to 110 mV and the DC
bias from −1.6 to 0.6 V. TRPL measurements were per-
formed on the devices and the bottom of the CZTS
absorber using time‐correlated single‐photon counting
(Microtime200, Picoquant), with excitation wavelengths
of 470 and 640 nm, respectively.
