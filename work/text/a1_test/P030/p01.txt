Photonic nanostructures mimicking ﬂoral
epidermis for perovskite solar cells
Here, Vasilopoulou et al. report petal-inspired light-polymerized nanocoatings
with light trapping, water repellence, and UV light and environmental moisture
protection properties in perovskite solar cells (PSCs). The coatings increase power
conversion efﬁciency to 24.61% and enable stable operation under AM 1.5G and
UV light continuous illumination.
