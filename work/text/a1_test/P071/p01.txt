1.Introduction
Organic-inorganichybridleadhalideper-
ovskite materials have emerged as com-
petitive candidates for next-generation
photovoltaics and optoelectronics.[1–3] To
address the intrinsic environmental in-
stability of conventional 3D perovskites,
long-chain organic spacer cations are
introducedtoderivethe2DRuddlesden-
Popper phase perovskites (RPPs) with
enhanced tolerance to moisture and
oxygen.[4–7] The general composition of
2D RPPs is R2An−1PbnX3n+1,wherein
X corresponds to the halide anions, A
is the short-chain organic cation, and R
represents the long-chain spacer cations
that sandwich n layers of [PbX6]4−
octahedra.[8,9] Perovskite solar cells
basedon2DRPPsﬁlms(2D-PSCs)have
demonstrated improved operational
stability compared to those based on 3D
perovskites (3D-PSCs).[10] However, the
presence of insulating spacer cations
within 2D perovskite ﬁlms and the
quantum conﬁnement eﬀects of 2D RPPs inevitably complicate
the phase distribution proﬁle and energy landscapes, leading to
thelowerPCEfor2D-PSCscomparedto3D-PSCs.[11] Thetrans-
portofphoto-generatedexcitonsislimitedwithinthe2Dplanes
ofRPPsslabs,withexcitonstendingtomigratefromRPPswith
smallslabthickness(small-n phase,largebandgap)towardthick
RPPs(large-n phase,smallbandgap).[12] Therefore,aperfectver-
ticalorientationof2DRPPsslabsandanorganizedphasedistri-
butionof2Dperovskitesarecrucialforachievingeﬃcientcharge
transport and collection along the vertical direction required by
high-performance2D-PSCs.[13]
Many strategies have been explored in the past few years to
pursue the vertical orientation and ordered alignment of 2D
RPPs slabs within 2D perovskite ﬁlms, leading to a signiﬁcant
PCE enhancement of 2D-PSCs from below 10% to approach-
ing 21%.[10,14,15] These include solvent engineering,[16] additive
incorporation,[17] compositional design,[18,19] and the manipula-
tion of the interfacial properties. Despite the eﬀectiveness of
