In addition to the tests mentioned above, stability assessments of the

above 89% of its initial performance after the same duration, as
shown in Fig. S5 of the supplementary material.

layer, the Mott–Schottky plot was utilized to assess the change in
the built-in potential [Vbi, Fig. 2(a)]. The Vbi in the operating state
of the device can be determined from the computational fit of the
equation20 [Eq. (1)],
