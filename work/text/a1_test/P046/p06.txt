When using the wide-gap device as a top cell in a tandem con-
figuration, it would be desirable to ‘flip’ the entire structure
upside down and illuminate through the rear side (SLG/IOW/
etc.). In this way, a glass sheet could be saved. However, due
to the low diffusion length of the wide-gap chalcopyrite material,
the carrier collection is much worse at rear as compared to
front illumination. As a consequence, the JSC bifaciality factor
(JSC,bif =
Jrear SC,qe
Jfront SC,qe ) was found to be limited to ≈60% for wide-gap
ACIGS solar cells with dabs ≥ 1 μm[49, 50]. Figure 3a shows
the EQE spectra at front and rear illumination for all samples
with a TBC from this study (only best cells). The corresponding
JSC,bif values can be found in Table 2. Excluding the cell without
RbF-PDT and thin CdS, the collection at rear illumination
improves with RbF dose. This would indicate an increased diffu-
sion length by the Rb incorporation. However, since the best JSC,
bif value of 72% is measured for just this sample without RbF and
thin CdS, evidence for such a trend cannot be claimed. When
comparing Cu-containing (AAC ≈ 0.8) cells from our previous
work to the AIGS samples with similar EG and the same device
structure from this work, the bifaciality is roughly on the same
level (see Figure 3b). However, the best JSC,bif of 72% from this
work is outstanding for an absorber thickness >1 μm. Samples
which show strong blocking in JV that reduces the Jfront SC,qe value
(like for 20 nm RbF-PDT in this study) are excluded in Figure 3b.
2.2 | Material Characterization
To understand why the RbF-PDT does not lead to an improve-
ment of any photovoltaic parameter when applied to our
wide-gap AIGS absorber, the treatment-induced chemical and
structural changes are presented and discussed in this section.
In the first part 2.2.1., surface modifications after RbF-PDT
are illustrated and the second part 2.2.2. analyzes the effect of
the Rb incorporation underneath the absorber surface (i.e., front
interface, bulk, grain boundaries, and back interface).
