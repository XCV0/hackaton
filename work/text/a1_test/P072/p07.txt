In addition, the results of the efﬁciency variation versus carrier lifetime are depicted
in Figure 4. As shown in Figure 4, the efﬁciency was increased as a result of an increase in
carrier lifetime. The value of the carrier lifetime was chosen to be similar across different
layers of the proposed solar cell (100 ns) over the entire simulation. Therefore, the carrier
lifetime is an important parameter in solar cell design that affects the improvement of
performance as well [36–40].
