Moreover, by decreasing the absorber layer, the optical properties were decreased. As
illustrated in Figure 5b, the EQE of the solar cell was decreased in lower wavelengths for
case 1. Likewise, the electrical properties of case 1 declined by decreasing the width of the
absorber layer, as demonstrated in Figure 5b. Thus, by selecting the optimum width of the
absorber layer, more advantages will be obtained. In this regard, over the entire simulation,
the optimum value of 2 microns was chosen for the absorber layer for the different cases.
