We also investigated the effect of defects at the interface
between the ETL and CsPbBr3 layer. In the simulations, a
CsPbBr3 layer with a Gaussian-shaped defect with a defect
density of 1 /C2 1019 cm/C03 was used. The peak position of the
Gaussian defect was 1.19 eV, which is half the bandgap of the
CsPbBr3 layer, whereas the energy width of the Gaussian defect
was 0.1 eV. The thickness and carrier mobility of the CsPbBr3
layer were ﬁxed at 300 nm and 10 cm2 Vs/C01, respectively.
