This study allowed us to optimize the solar cell
having the structure ZnO/CdS/CZTS/MoS2 and
to obtain an eﬃciency of 17.93%. The best per-
formances are obtained for a low CdS layer thick-
ness of only 15 nm, which is advantageous, thanks
to the reduction in the quantity of cadmium used
for the buﬀer layer elaboration. The optimal CdS
gap energy was set at 3.2 eV. This large gap value
can be obtained by doping the CdS with oxy-
gen [18, 23–25].
These ﬁndings are very promising and can pro-
vide helpful guidance for experimentalists in the
manufacture of high-performance CZTS solar cells.
