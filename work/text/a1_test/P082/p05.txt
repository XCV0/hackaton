XRD measurement was performed for the CdS films deposited on CZTSSe 
at 55, 60, 65, 70 and 75 ℃, as shown in Fig. 5. In order to form a contrast 
better, CZTSSe without CdS was also involved in the XRD measurement 
as a control, as shown in Fig. 5(f). From Fig. 5(f), only one strong and 
weak XRD peaks are observed at 27.25◦ and 28.41◦, respectively, for 
CZTSSe film, which are due to diffraction of (112) and (103) planes of 
CZTSSe. However, for the CZTSSe films with CdS, a shoulder is observed 
at the left side of the strong peak, except for the two peaks of CZTSSe. In 
order to determine peak position of the shoulder, diffraction peak near 
27.25◦ is fitted with Gaussian mode for the five samples, and the fitting 
results are listed in Table 5. As shown in Fig. 5(g)-(k), it is found that the 
peak is well fitted by three sub-peaks. The strong peak located at 27.25◦
is due to kesterite CZTSSe. The peak at left side of the strong peak 
located at about 26.86◦ is assigned to diffraction angles of the (111) 
plane of the cubic phase or the (002) plane of the hexagonal phase of 
CdS [32], since the difference in the interplanar crystal spacing between 
the (111) plane of the cubic phase and the (002) plane of the hexagonal 
phase is too small to distinguish whether it is due to cubic phase or the 
hexagonal phase [32]. This confirms that these particles consist of CdS 
nanocrystal. In addition, the peak at right side of the strong peak located 
at 27.37◦, which is between diffraction angles of (111) plane of ZnS 
(JCPDS card no. 05–0566) and ZnSe (JCPDS card no. 37–1463). Many 
literatures have demonstrated that a small amount of secondary phases 
is easily formed in CZTSSe film due to that composition range of single 
phase of kesterite CZTSSe is very small and composition prepared 
CZTSSe film deviate from this range easily, and the secondary phase is 
often Zn(S,Se) [33], [34,35]. So, it is deduced that the peak located at 
27.37◦ is ascribed to diffraction of the (111) plane of Zn(S,Se). This 
means that there is second phase in the absorber. The formation of the 
Zn(S,Se) implies that the composition of the CZTSSe absorber deviates 
from stoichiometric ratio of single kesterite phase of CZTSSe [33].
