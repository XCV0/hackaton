4. Conclusions 
The CZTSSe solar cells with conventional structure, CdS-x-cells (x =
55, 60,65,70 and 75), were fabricated by using CdS deposited at tem-
peratures of 55, 60, 65, 70 and 75 ℃, respectively. It’s found that the 
PCE of the h-CdS-x-cells rises from 8.52% to 10.36% as the temperature 
increases from 55 to 60 ℃, but decreases from 10.36% to 7.14% from 60 
to 75 ℃. 
It is deduced by calculation that the increase in PCE from h-CdS-55- 
cell to h-CdS-60-cell comes predominantly from contribution of 
decreased J0, followed by reduced RS and increased RSh. The decrease in 
PCE from h-CdS-60-cell to h-CdS-75-cell comes predominantly from 
increased J0, followed by decrease in RSh and JL. Effect of the change of 
J0 with deposition temperature on PCE is much larger than that of JL, RS 
and RSh. 
The increase or decrease in J0 with the deposition temperature 
results mainly from decrease or increase in Vbi, which is determined by 
decreased Eg and increased ND of CdS and decreased NA of CZTSSe with 
increasing deposition temperature. The decreased RS is attributed to 
decreasd resistivity of CdS due to improvement of crystal quality of CdS 
with increment of deposition temperature. JL is determined by Wd and 
photon number passing through CdS layer, the former increases with 
increasing deposition temperature due to increased ND of CdS, and the 
latter decreases due to decrease in the Eg and transmittance of CdS. 
According to above results, it is concluded that influence of deposi-
tion temperature on PCE comes from the decreased Eg due to increased 
grain size of CdS, the improved ND due to increased Cd/S ratio, the 
decreased NA due to increase of Cd content diffused into CZTSSe with 
increasing deposition temperature, and reduced resistivity of CdS due to 
improvement of its crystal quality.
