Surprisingly, the bandgap values of control films grown on
different substrates vary within the range of 1.57−1.60 eV. It is
worth noting that these films were deposited during the same
depositionrun,andassuch,theirbandgapsshould beidentical,
making a 30 meV difference in the bandgap significantly larger
than one would expect. This lends credence to the hypothesis
that variations in the composition of the first few nm of
perovskite can indeed affect the stoichiometry of the bulk
material.34 Encouragingly, however, we note that with the
inclusion of a templating layer the absorption coefficient and
bandgap of the deposited films (1.59 eV) are consistent,
regardless of the nature of the underlying substrate. This
suggests that with the templating layer, we are able to
reproducibly control the crystallization and composition of the
perovskite.
