As illustrated in Fig. S7, the peaks of Pb 4 f shift to a lower 
binding energy after TBP treatment, revealing that TBP inter-
acts with the center Pb to form a complex [38]. Accordingly, 
the TBP-induced degradation mechanism can be concluded as 
the following reactions:
(1)FA1-xMAxPbI3(s) ↔ FAI (s) + MAI (s) + PbI2(s)
(2)PbI2(s) + xTBP (s) → PbI2 - TBPx (aq)
(3)PbI2(s) + xTBP (s) → /bracketleft.s1PbI2 ⋅ xTBP/bracketright.s1 (s)
The formation of  PbI2-TBP complex will further induce 
the perovskite decomposition according to Le Chatelier’s 
principle. With this mechanism, a large number of defects 
generates on the perovskite surface, which is also the main 
route of ion migration [39]. The device degradation can be 
more severe even with advanced sealing techniques.
