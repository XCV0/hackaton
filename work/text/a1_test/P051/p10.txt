vs. 65% at 60 ± 5% RH and 72% vs. 41% at about 85% RH 
after 240 h).
4  Conclusion
In summary, we developed a chemical binding strategy 
via halogen bonds to suppress the TBP-induced instabil-
ity in PSCs. 1,4-DIFTB was applied to interact with the 
N atom in TBP. After the formation of 1,4-DITFB-TBP 
complex, the decomposition of perovskites is significantly 
alleviated, and the de-doping of spiro is suppressed. The
