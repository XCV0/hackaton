The ex-situ Raman and EDX measurements suggest that the Ge incorporation into
kesterite does not signiﬁcantly inﬂuence the reported formation path of the kesterite
phase [11,12]. In the case of Ge incorporation, the Cu-rich to Cu-poor composition shift
occurs at a slightly lower temperature (480 ◦C to 530 ◦C) due to the higher vapor pressure
of GeSe2−x as compared to SnSe2−x (see Figure S1). This means that up to this temperature
the process is unaltered and all reaction steps and pre-steps of the kesterite formation are
not inﬂuenced.
