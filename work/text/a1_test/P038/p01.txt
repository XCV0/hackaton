MAPbI3 grains and their photovoltaic responses
A suitable drying time of the perovskite precursor solution 
can facilitate the formation of merged grains while 
maintaining the interfacial contact quality between the 
perovskite thin ﬁ lm and hole transport layer, which highly 
increases the open-circuit voltage (VOC) from 1.01 V to 1.15 V

higher than 1.15 V after 80 days.
