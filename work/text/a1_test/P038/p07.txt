intensities is proportional to the trend of the I110/I002 ratio
values (see Table 2). When the drying time is 30 s, the PL
intensity from the top region of the MAPbI3 crystalline thin lm
corresponds to the highest value. When the drying time is
reduced to 20 s, the relatively lower PL intensity can be
explained as due to the higher defect density at the inter-gain
boundaries of the MAPbI3 crystalline thin lm. When the
drying time is extended to 40 s, the relatively lower PL intensity
can be explained as due to the higher surface defect density of
the rough MAPbI3 crystalline thin lm. Fig. 8 presents the
absorbance spectra of the MAPbI3/P3CT-Na/ITO/glass samples
fabricated with diﬀerent drying times. In the near-infrared
wavelength range from 800 nm to 1050 nm, the formation of
ripples is due to the thin-lm interference between interfaces.
The larger amplitude of the interference ripple indicates the
weaker light scattering from the surface due to the lower
roughness of the MAPbI3 thin lms.57,58 The trend of the ripple
amplitudes is inversely proportional to the trend of the Ra
values (see Fig. 5). Besides, the higher absorbance is due to the
larger Ra when the drying time is 40 s. In general, the slope of
the absorbance curve in the continuum absorption band is
inversely proportional to the exciton binding energy of the
MAPbI3 crystalline thin lm.59 It is noted that the trend of the
exciton binding energies is inversely proportional to the trends
of the JSC values and FF values (see Table 1). In other words, the
higher PCE of the inverted perovskite photovoltaic cells is also
related to the lower exciton binding energy of the MAPbI3
crystalline thin lm due to the formation of merged and so
perovskite grains.
Fig. 9 presents the reectance spectra and bottom-excited PL
spectra of the MAPbI3/P3CT-Na/ITO/glass samples before and
aer the encapsulation process. The MAPbI3 crystalline thin
lms are fabricated with diﬀerent drying times. The reectance
peak is related to the exciton transition of the MAPbI3 crystal-
line thin lm. According to the material dispersion relation,
a longer exciton transition wavelength results in a longer
reectance peak wavelength due to the higher refractive index.60
Therefore, the diﬀerence between the reectance peak wave-
length and the PL peak wavelength (Dl = lPL − lR) is propor-
tional to the Stokes shi (exciton binding energy). The
reectance peak wavelengths, PL peak wavelengths and Dl
values of the MAPbI3/P3CT-Na/ITO/glass samples are listed in
Table 3. Before encapsulation, the trend of the Dl (exciton
binding energy) values is proportional to the trend of the grain
sizes (see Fig. 5(d)–(f)), which means that the formation of
merged and so grains results in lattice distortion, thereby
increasing the Dl (exciton binding energy) in the bottom region
of the MAPbI3 crystalline thin lm.61 In other words, the
extended drying time results in a distorted lattice in the bottom
region of the MAPbI3 crystalline thin lm, thereby decreasing
the atomic contact strength at the MAPbI3/P3CT-Na interface
(see Fig. 5(d) and (e)). In the aspect of the device performance of
un-encapsulated photovoltaic cells on the rst day, the average
VOC value increases from 1.010 V to 1.017 V with the increase in
the drying time from 20 s to 40 s, which can be explained due to
the increased grain size (decreased potential loss). However, the
average JSC (FF) value has the highest value when the drying
time is 30 s, which is due to the better contact quality at the
MAPbI3/P3TC-Na interface and the lower exciton binding
energy of the MAPbI3 thin lm.
Aer encapsulation, the decreased Dl values are 2.4, 5.8 and
1.9 nm when the drying times are 20, 30 and 40 s, respectively. It
is noted that the trend of the decreased Dl (exciton binding
energy) values is proportional to the trend of the increased VOC
values (see Table 1) aer encapsulation. The decreased Dl
(exciton binding energy) in the bottom region of the MAPbI3
crystalline thin lm can be explained as due to the formation of
shallow defects aer the encapsulation process, which
increases the eﬀective Fermi level for electrons of the so
MAPbI3 crystalline thin lm, thereby resulting in the higher VOC
of the inverted perovskite photovoltaic cells.
Conclusions
In summary, the open-circuit voltage of the inverted perovskite
crystalline photovoltaic cells can be largely increased from
1.010 V to 1.156 V via the formation of merged and so CH3-
NH3PbI3 (MAPbI3) grains and the reduced exciton binding
energy (increased carrier density), which are conrmed by
analyzing the surface morphologies, cross-sectional images,
crystal structures and excitonic properties of the MAPbI3/P3CT-
Na/ITO/glass samples with and without encapsulation. It is
noted that the photovoltaic cells maintain the average power
conversion eﬃciency to be higher than 17.5% within 80 days
when the MAPbI3 thin lm is fabricated with an optimized
drying time of 30 s.
