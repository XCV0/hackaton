Non-selective adsorption of organic cations
enables conformal surface capping of
perovskite grains for stabilized photovoltaic
operation
Taking advantage of the weak-anisotropic adsorption nature of tertiary ammonium
on perovskite facets, Zhou et al. demonstrate a comprehensive protection of
perovskite grain surface by constructing a conformal low-dimensional perovskite
passivation layer. The inhibited ion migration enables stabilized solar cell
operation.
