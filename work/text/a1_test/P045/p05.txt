A surface treatment involving O2 plasma is performed on both
the substrate surface and the PDMS device for the individualized
NPs deposition.[68] The O2 plasma treatment improves the device
adhesion to the surface by eliminating surface contaminants and
introducing reactive chemical groups.[78,79] Following the plasma
treatment, the PDMS device was manually afﬁxed to the sub-
strate and subjected to annealing at 65 °C for 20 min. The Au
NPs solution was then injected into the microﬂuidic device
and allowed to dry for 2 h, followed by the removal of the
PDMS device from the substrate. The Au NPs deposition proce-
