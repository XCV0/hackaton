4. Conclusion
In summary, we successfully synthesize single-atom Ni doped
graphene by the impregnation method using Ni(NO3)2 solution
and applied it to construct composite electrode for efﬁcient planar
carbon-based PSCs. Ni doping can decrease the work function of
graphene from 5.29 to 5.02 eV, which enhances the hole extraction
from the perovskite to composite electrode. Compared with the
controlled PSCs, the average PCE of PSCs base on Ni-NG/CE was
optimized from 8.71% to 10.90% and a champion PCE of 12.39%
was obtained. To the best of our knowledge, it is ﬁrst reported that
single-atom doped carbon material is applied to improve the per-
formance of PSCs. We believe this carbon material has wide appli-
cation for PSCs through further optimization.
