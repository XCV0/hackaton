3. Conclusions
A novel photocationic initiator TPFB was introduced into perov-
skite ﬁlms through antisolvent process. The hypervalent iodine
part of TPFB served as a Lewis acid to passivate the negatively
charged defects. Meanwhile, the pentaﬂuorophenyl borate part
tailored the crystallization process through the formation of
hydrogen bonding and increased hydrophobicity of perovskite
ﬁlms. This led to substantially enhanced efﬁciency and environ-
mental stability due to multifunctional passivation mechanisms.
The maximum PCE of MAPbI3-based devices reached 20.41%
with a Voc up to 1.15 V. The device also retained 85% of its initial
efﬁciency after 1000 h shelf storage. Meanwhile, the CsFA-based
PSCs sample obtained using TPFB through posttreatment pro-
cess delivered a PCE of 23.38%. In sum, these ﬁndings opened
up a novel Lewis acid paradigm through a simple doping process,
which might be promising for the commercialization of PSCs.
