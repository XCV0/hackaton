Interestingly, HI is smaller in those devices without any pre-
treatment (with/without CuSCN post-treatment). With the
presence of CuSCN, HI is slightly smaller than that of Ag3BiI6
only devices, indicating eﬃcient charge extraction. Although
CuSCN may re-dissolve Ag3BiI6, remaining Ag+ in the meso-
porous layer could potentially create more defects, obstructing
the charge separation by charge accumulation at interfaces.
Stability was evaluated for all fabricated solar cells, following
the ambient dark storage protocol of the International Summit
on OPV stability (ISOS) protocol—ISOS-D-1.40 The performance
of all devices was recorded for 99 days, and consistent eﬃciency
with the initial devices was found. Fig. 8 presents the one-week
device performance regarding the ageing eﬀect, and the corre-
sponding device parameters are summarised in Table 1.
Fig. 8 and Table 1 show that the performance of all devices
increased aer one-week ageing (more details in Fig. S7 and
Table S2†). Slow solvent evaporation and crystallisation process
are likely to be the underlying factors to improve Voc and Jsc.Itis
worth noting that the eﬃciency of the champion CuSCN treated
device doubled from 0.33% (day 1) to 0.60% (day 7) and peaked
at 0.74% on day 28 (Table S2†). Besides, the HI of the CuSCN-
treated devices reduced aer 14 days, from 0.30 to 0.25. When
comparing the dark storage stability parameters (Fig. 9 and
S8†), the increased PCE is mainly due to Jsc improvement where
Voc and FF results maintain a stable level. The distinct perfor-
mance decay aer day 80 is mainly caused by fatigue in the
devices due to repeated impedance measurements. Neverthe-
less, the overall results indicate satisfactory ageing stability,
also suggesting that better device eﬃciency could be obtained
by improving the initial performance.
