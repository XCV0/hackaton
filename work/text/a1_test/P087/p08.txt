(DMSO, 99.9%) and chlorobenzene (CB, 99.8%) were all purchased from
Sigma Aldrich (USA). Glass substrates were purchased from Suzhou
Shangyang Solar Technology Co. Ltd (China). The PET substrates (~12
μm thickness) were purchased from Shanghai Yiwei Mechanical &
Electrical Hardware Co. Ltd (china).
To prepare the Al2O3 dispersion, 1 mL IPA was added into 8 μLAl2O3
colloid and oscillated to achieve a suitable concentration. The resulting
Al2O3 dispersion can be stored for several weeks and should be evenly
oscillated before use. It is important to note that the appropriate con-
centration of Al2O3 is closely related to the substrate roughness. An in-
crease in RS and decrease in FF may be attributed to the deposition of
thicker Al2O3 on relatively smooth surfaces.
4.2. Device fabrication
PET and glass substrates were washed with deionized water, ethanol,
and isopropanol sequentially. The substrates were dried with N2. Sub-
sequently, PDMS (was obtained by mixing the elastomer and curing
agent in 10:1 and diluted 1:2 in hexane) was spin-coated on the glass
substrates at 2000 rpm for 40 s and annealed at 100 /C14C for 5 min as the
binder for the PET foil and glass substrate. A dispersion of PEDOT:PSS
was prepared from stock Clevios PH1000 with 5 vol% DMSO and 0.5 vol
% Zonyl FS-300 ﬂuorosurfactant. This formulation was spun onto the PET
foil or the glass substrate at 2000 rpm for 40 s, and subsequently
annealed at 120 /C14C for 10 min. The PEDOT:PSS ﬁlms were brieﬂy rinsed
with methanol to remove any excess surfactant and PSS on the surface,
and dried for another 10 min at 120 /C14C. The UF-substrated was trans-
ferred to a waterless air glove box, and PTAA (2 mg⋅mL/C01) was coated at
5000 rpm for 30 s and annealed at 100 /C14C for 10min. For DMF PW, 100 μL
DMF solution was prewashed on the substrate and spun at 4000 rpm for
30s. For Al2O3 SM, Al2O3 (0.16 wt%) was coated on the surface of PTAA
at 3000 rpm for 30 s and annealed for 15 min. The 1.5 M Cs0.05FA0.85-
MA0.1PbI2.91Br0.09 perovskite precursor was prepared by mixing 0.075 M
CsI, 1.275 M FAI, 0.15 M MABr and 1.6 M PbI2 in 1 mL DMF and DMSO
(4:1, v/v); an extra 0.2 M MACl was added to the precursor. The 35 μL
perovskite precursor solution was spread on the Al2O3-coated substrate
and spun at 1000 rpm at a ramp rate of 200 rpm⋅s/C01 for 10 s and 4000
rpm at the ramp rate of 2000 rpm⋅s/C01 for 30 s; then 240 μL of ethyl ac-
etate was quickly dripped onto the substrate in the last 5 s. The perovskite
layer was then annealed at 70 /C14C for 1 min and 110 /C14C for 10 min.
Subsequently, the ETL was dynamically deposited from a PCBM solution
(20 mg⋅mL/C01 in CB) and spin-coated onto the perovskite layer at the
speed of 1500 rpm for 30 s. Next, The BCP solution (0.5 mg⋅mL/C01 in IPA)
was spin-coated at 4000 rpm for 30 s on top of the PCBM layer. The 3 M
copper tape was applied to adhere to the electrode via silver glue. Finally,
an 80 nm Ag electrode was thermally evaporated on top of the device.
