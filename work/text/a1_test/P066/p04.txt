We then investigated the eﬀectiveness of diﬀer-
ent XF treatment on the performance of PSCs. The
PSC adopts a conﬁguration of stacking layer of
ITO/SnO2/(FA0.98MA0.02)0.95Cs0.05Pb(I0.95Br0.05)3/spiro-
MeOTAD/Au where the SnO2 was treated without or with
KF, RbF, or CsF. In the following, the device fabricated on
SnO2 treated with KF, RbF, and CsF are named as SnO2:KF,
SnO2:RbF, and SnO2:CsF, respectively. Details on fabrication
of the PSC device are shown in the supporting information. A
cross-sectional SEM image of a typical device is presented in
Figure 2a. Comparing the I–V performance of the PSCs, we
have found that the PSCs with all the XF treatments including
KF, RbF, and Cs show improved eﬃciency. Among them, the
SnO2:RbFdevicedeliversthebestperformance.Figure2bshows
the I–V curves of the best devices. The PCE of the SnO2:RbF
device reaches to 20.84% with Voc = 1.10 V, Jsc = 24.51 mA
cm−2,andFF= 77.55%.Thisperformanceisfollowedbydevice
with CsF treatment with PCE of 20.64% with Voc = 1.06 V, Jsc
= 24.37 mA cm−2,andFF= 80.23%. The SnO2:KF devices
also achieve an enhanced PCE of 20.40%, Voc of 1.08 V, Jsc of
24.47 mA cm−2, and FF of 76.85%. These device performances
outperform that of the device using pristine SnO2 which only
produce a maximum PCE of 19.72% with Voc = 1.06 V, Jsc =
24.46mAcm−2,andFF= 75.91%.Thestatisticaldiagramscom-
paring the PCE of the devices with and without XF treatment is
showninFigure2candcomparisonofotherstatisticalcharacter-
istic performance parameters (Voc, Jsc, and FF) of these devices
is shown in Figure S6, Supporting Information. It is worth to
notethatallthedeviceswithorwithouttreatmentexhibitasmall
hysteresis (Figure S7, Supporting Information) The hysteresis
index HI (HI = (PCEReverse − PCEForward)/PCEReverse)ofSnO2,
SnO2:KF,SnO2:RbF,andSnO2:CsFdeviceswere1%,1%,0.5%,
and 0.8%, respectively. Overall, the alkali salts treatment on
SnO2 resultsinapositiveeﬀectonthePCEofthecorresponding
PSCs.
