The JSC values are more signiﬁcantly impacted by both com-
positions, with peak at /C2516% Mg and total Se/(Se þ Te) of 4–6%
(Figure 2b). For a ﬁxed total Se composition of 7.3%, the JSC
improves with increasing Mg in MgxZn1/C0xO, due to increased
carrier collection at short (blue) wavelengths (Figure 3c) as the
bandgap of MgxZn1/C0xO increases. For ﬁxed Mg composition
of 4.0%, JSC improves with increasing Se in CdSeyTe1/C0y due
to bandgap lowering and hence improves carrier collection at
longer (red) wavelengths (Figure 3d). The ﬁll factor of the devices
is most impacted by MgxZn1/C0xO composition. Greater Mg
results in reduced ﬁll factor, which may be associated with higher
MgxZn1/C0xO ﬁlm resistivity at high Mg compositions.[31]
Together, VOC, JSC, and FF are impacted differently by the
MgxZn1/C0xO and CdSeyTe1/C0y compositions (Figure 2).
Although higher compositions are beneﬁcial for JSC, the efﬁ-
ciency is reduced by lower VOC and FF. The result is a compli-
cated efﬁciency map, with peaks occurring at multiple locations,
as shown in Figure 2d and highlighted in Figure S6, Supporting
Information. Table S1, Supporting Information lists solar cell
parameters for some of the better performing devices. The
highest observed VOC was 842 mV, at compositions of
Mg/(Mg þ Zn) /C259% and total Se/(Se þ Te) /C253.8%. The highest
observed JSC was 30.6 mA cm/C02, which also corresponded to rel-
atively high efﬁciency of 17.7% despite a modest FF of 69%. This
occurred at compositions of Mg/(Mg þ Zn) /C2518% and total
Se/(Se þ Te) /C254%, with interface Se/(Se þ Te) /C2523% from
AES data. Optimizing device fabrication parameters such as
Cl heat treatment and Cu backcontact at the best absorber–
contact combination space could lead to further improvement
of efﬁciency above 18%.
Carrier concentration estimated from capacitance–voltage
measurements (Figure S7, Supporting Information) indicate a
decrease in electron density in 1015 cm/C03 range by /C252x with
increasing Se content, consistent with the tendency of II–VI
semiconductors with Se or S anions toward n-type doping.
For total Se content ﬁxed at /C257.0%, increasing Mg increases
the depletion width slightly (/C2520%), though it is difﬁcult to con-
clusively state that this trend is caused by actual change in carrier
density (/C2540%) rather than variations in the CV measurements.
The vertical rise of the carrier concentration near the back of the
device is indicative of the full depletion through the entire thick-
ness of the absorber to the back contact. Indeed, the thickness at
which the carrier density increases (3.5–4.5 μm) is similar to the
nominal absorber thickness (/C255 μm).
