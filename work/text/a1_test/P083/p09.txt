4. Conclusions
In conclusion, the successful fabrication of CdTe NC solar cells with 2-iodothiophene
treatment has been achieved by meticulously controlling the concentration of the 2-
iodothiophene methanol solution. The formation of a Te-rich layer subsequent to 2-
iodothiophene treatment leads to diminished carrier recombination at the interface and
reduced contact resistance. CdTe NC thin-film devices subjected to 2-iodothiophene treat-
ment exhibit enhanced power conversion efficiencies (PCEs) of up to 4.31%, surpassing
those of untreated and NH4I-treated devices. The superior performance of these devices
is predominantly attributed to the reduced recombination losses at the CdTe/Au back
electrode interface, as substantiated by an array of electrical and optical assessments. Addi-
tionally, devices treated with 2-iodothiophene demonstrate exceptional operational stability,
exhibiting no degradation under ambient conditions. This study underscores the efficacy
of this straightforward 2-iodothiophene treatment method, which holds promise for in-
tegration into future manufacturing systems for low-cost, solution-processed CdTe NC
solar cells.
