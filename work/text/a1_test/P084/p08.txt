CdS Deposition:A/C2560 nm CdS thin ﬁlm was deposited via chemical bath
deposition (CBD).[30] Substrates were immersed in a glass container with
750 mL aqueous solution of cadmium acetate dihydrate (1.3 g) and ammo-
nium acetate (1.5 g) at 65 °C with magnetic stirring. CdS ﬁlm thickness was
controlled by adjusting the deposition time. For /C2560 nm CdS, the deposi-
tion was started by adding a thiourea solution (0.74 g in 32.5 mL of 25%
ammonia) and stopped after 10 min by removing the substrates and placing
them in Milli-Q water. This /C2560 nm CdS deposition was used as the stan-
dard for all devices compared in this manuscript. An additional 100 nm CdS
ﬁlm, used for comparative work, was achieved by increasing the deposition
time to 16 min. Films were then ultrasonicated for 60 s in Milli-Q water and
then for a further 30 s in HCl (pH 3) to remove surface residues and ﬁnally
rinsed with Milli-Q and isopropanol before drying with an N2 gun.
CdS Post-Treatment: After deposition, CdS was treated with H2O2 and
CdCl2. The H2O2 treatment was done by dropping H2O2 30% (500 μL) on
ﬁlms spinning at 3000 RMP for 30 s. Films were then rinsed with Milli-Q
water and dried using an N2 gun. Next, the ﬁlms were treated with CdCl2 by
spinning a warm supersaturated solution of CdCl2.H2O in methanol
(100 μL, 20 mg mL/C01) at 4000 RPM for 30 s followed by annealing for
/C255 min at 400 °C in air.
Deposition of Sb2Se3: Vapor transport deposition (VTD) was used to
deposit Sb2Se3 in adapted MTI dual-zone furnace OTF-1200X-II with a
quartz tube. A quartz crucible containing 500 mg of Sb2Se3 powder
was put inside the tube in the center of the left heating zone.
Previously prepared substrates were mounted on a near-vertical graphite
holder that was placed in the right heating zone. VTD was performed by
heating both zones at 250 °C in an N2 purged vacuum atmosphere at
0.5–0.7 Pa for 30 min. Then, only the zone containing the source was
heated (20 °C min/C01) to 540 °C and the temperature was held for
10 min. The zone with the substrates was maintained at 250 °C throughout
the whole deposition process. The furnace was then allowed cool naturally
to /C25300 °C before ambient pressure was restored by backﬁlling the tube
with N2 and the furnace lid was opened to allow faster cooling. Further
details on this process are provided in the supporting information.
Instrumentation: The SEM images were taken on an FEI Verios 460 L
XHRMO-SEM or FEI Nova NanoSEM 200. XRD patterns were obtained
using a Bruker AXS D4 Endeavour diffractometer with a Cu-Kα radiation
source. XPS were collected on a Kratos AXIS Supra XPS with a monochro-
mated aluminum Kα source (1486.7 eV). Film thickness was determined
using a Tencor P 16 þ Proﬁler.
Solar Cell Fabrication and Measurements: Au back contacts were depos-
ited on the Sb2Se3 layer using an e-beam evaporator. Current density ver-
sus voltage measurements were conducted on a customized setup with
Keithley 2401 Source Meter. Nyquist plots were obtained from electro-
chemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) measurements done using an
Ivium Vertex.1A.EIS potentiostat at /C00.8 V in a frequency interval varying
from 200 kHz to 1 Hz. Simulated AM1.5G light was produced in an Oriel
Sol3A Class AAA Solar Simulator, and solar cell efﬁciency was determined
following the guidelines for the best measurement technique,[31] using
a laser-cut mask (0.100 cm2)todeﬁne the device area. Quasi-
monochromatic light was generated in a PVM QEX10 Solar Cell
Quantum Efﬁciency Measurement System. ThorLabs LEDs were used
to produce red (625 nm–M625L3), green (530 nm–M530L3), and blue
(455 nm–M455L3) light. Simulations performed using SCAPS parameters
for Sb2Se3 solar cell simulation were based on established literature values
and are shown in Table S1, Supporting Information.[8b,32]
