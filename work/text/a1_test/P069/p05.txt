much easier extraction of the photo-generated carrier (i.e., elec-
trons at the electron-selective contact).
Temperature-dependent Voc characteristics are shown in
Figure 5. The electrical measurements were carried from
300 K down to 100 K, sampling every 25 K. At room temperature,
the Voc experiences a linear decrease. The extrapolated values of
activation energy (Ea) for all the studied devices are presented in
Table 2. The extrapolated Ea serves as an indicator of the maxi-
mum achievable quasi-Fermi level separation. If Ea approaches
the absorber bandgap, Voc is dominated by bulk recombination
and thus interface recombination becomes negligible.
Conversely, when Ea is signiﬁcantly smaller than the bandgap,
a substantial portion of total recombination is expected to take
place at the interfaces. For the bandgap of 1.7 eV in a-Si:H,
one would expect high Ea values in solar cells with optimum
interfaces. Thus, the low Ea value of the reference device suggests
that a high degree of recombination takes place at the interfaces.
In contrast, it seems evident from the higher Ea values that
interface recombination was signiﬁcantly mitigated for devices
incorporating dipole layers.
2.2. Spectral Response
Spectral response measurements were performed on the differ-
ent devices studied to compare and analyze their response.
In Figure 6, the EQE resulting from spectral response meas-
urements of the studied devices are presented. As already seen in
previous experimental work,[6,7] the response is centered at
around 400–430 nm. From the EQE, a calculation of the inte-
grated short-circuit current density ( Jsc) is performed to compare
this value with those extracted from the current density–voltage
( J–V ) measurements (Figure S9, Supporting Information).
When comparing the EQE of the different devices, it can be
concluded that the introduction of interfacial dipoles did not
affect the spectral response (hence, the Jsc) of the resulting devi-
ces. This fact is also supported when inspecting the J–V charac-
teristics of the different devices. Looking at the transmittance
spectra obtained from optical spectrophotometry, it is clear that
the changes coming from the incorporation of the interfacial
dipoles are negligible, and that indeed the most relevant change
is the strong increase in Voc and also a somewhat signiﬁcant
increase in the FF of the TPV devices, when compared against
the reference. Going back to the EQE measurements, it is con-
ﬁrmed that increased dipolar strength does not affect the spectral
response.
3. Conclusion
In conclusion, this study has addressed Voc deﬁcit.
Circumventing this issue by incorporating dipole layers in the
architecture using ultrathin a-Si absorber layers in TPV solar
cells, the results have shown a signiﬁcant improvement in the
performance of these devices, the most important coming from
a signiﬁcantly strong increase in the Voc and the FF, which has
led to a higher overall solar cell efﬁciency. The studied dipoles
PEI and PAMAM G1 and G2 have positively increased the Voc
