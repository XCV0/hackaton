5.2.Current–voltagehysteresis
Differences in the predictions of PSC behaviour between models based on Boltzmann statistics in the TLs
and those based on full FD statistics are not restricted to steady state behaviour. As shown in figures 7 and 8,
simulations of J-V hysteresis and IS both show significant differences depending on which of these two
modelling assumptions is applied.
