Measurements of the disorder parameter, s, for common organic PSC TL materials taken from the
literature are shown in table 1. Values range from 1.04 to 5.38. The corresponding diffusion enhancement
functions under the GER are plotted in figure 4. Even for the material with the least disorder (P3HT), the
GER prediction of ∆ has strongly diverged from 1 by the time the carrier density has increased to the typical
TL doping density. This divergence is far more pronounced for the more disordered materials (such as
PEDOT:PSS and PCBM) where it begins at relatively low carrier concentrations.
