indirect models, respectively, are in reasonable agreement with the
fitted bandgap trend. Increasing optical bandgap energies induced
by oxygen addition (up to 49 at. %), with both direct and indirect
bandgap evaluation, were also found by Jayaraman et al. for
In2(OxS1−x)3 layers grown by atomic-layer deposition (ALD).29
Barreau et al. reported for moderate oxygen addition (up to 8 at. %
corresponding to an OOS ratio of x = 0.14) to evaporated In2S3
thin films an increase of the bandgap, too.30
C. Band alignment between CIGS-based absorbers
and indium-based buffer materials
To get an insight into the band alignment between CIGS and
In2(OxS1−x)3 and to find a possible explanation for the decreasing
VOC values with oxygen addition [see Figs. 1(b) and 1(f )], DFT cal-
culations were performed. Figure 4 shows the calculated
bulk-intrinsic band alignment between CuInSe2 and CuIn5Se8
absorber materials with In2S3 and In2O3 buffer systems. The influ-
ence of gallium in CIGS was neglected in this calculation, but
gallium addition to CuInSe2 results in a slight increase of Eg due to
an increasing conduction band minimum (CBM), in our case for a
[Ga]/([Ga] + [In]) ratio around 0.3 from ∼1.0 to ∼1.2 eV. To
obtain the bulk-intrinsic band alignment between CuInSe2,
CuIn5Se8,In2S3, and In2O3, the In 3s core state was taken as the
reference core level energy, and the valence band offset was calcu-
lated as
ΔEv(A/B) ¼ ΔEv,c0 (B) /C0 ΔEv,c(A),
where ΔEv,c0 (B) and ΔEv,c(A) represent the energy separation
between the valence band maximum (VBM) and In 3s core level
energy of the bulk B and A compounds, respectively. The conduc-
tion band offset (CBO) was then obtained by using the relation
ΔEc(A/B) ¼ ΔEg(A/B) /C0 ΔEv(A/B),
where ΔEg(A/B) shows the difference between bandgaps of the two
compounds. As a global reference point, we choose the ionization
energy of the CuInSe2 (110) surface. We note that the common
alignment to the vacuum level is only strictly valid if the interface
is structurally ideal and if no gap states exist.31 Such gap states can
result from wave function coupling at the interface leading to
Fermi level pinning. In this case, the reference point for band align-
ment would be the charge neutrality level of the gap states. Based
on previous work for chalcogenide semiconductors,32 we expect
that the semiconductors treated in this work are sufficiently ionic
and no sizable interface dipole emerges. In this case, a common
vacuum level is well-defined.
The band positions show that the VBM of In2O3 is 2.63 eV
lower than that of In2S3. This is because the 2p orbitals of oxygen
are deeper in energy compared to the 3p orbitals of sulfur. Hence,
the pd-repulsion is larger for the anions with a smaller atomic
number and the VBM of In2S3 is markedly higher than that of
In2O3. We note that reduction of the CBM when moving from S to
O is associated with an increase in the volume of the S-compound
compared to the O-compound. This is similar to the trend
observed when moving from ZnO to ZnO0.5S0.5 and ZnS com-
pounds, as reported by Persson et al.33 For the device-relevant
oxygen contents up to an [O]/([O] + [S]) ratio of 0.25, we observe
smaller offsets of the VBM and CBM compared to the boundary
phases (see Fig. S5 in the supplementary material). However, the
course of the band offsets is not monotonous for the bixbyite crystal
structure. This crystal structure is, on the other hand, thermodynam-
ically unfavorable for low [O]/([O] + [S]) ratios (see Sec. III D).
According to our previous work,9 the CBO between the
Cu-poor absorber CuIn5Se8 and In2S3 is almost flat with a slightly
negative value of −0.07 eV and only after incorporation of Na to
the system and formation of NaIn5Se8 at the pn-junction, the CBO
between the CuIn5Se8 absorber and NaIn5Se8 buffer adopts a small
positive value (spike-like conformation) of +0.12 eV. For the CIGS
solar cells under investigation, taking the [Ga]/([Ga] + [In]) ratio
around 0.3 with a CBM shift of around +0.2 eV into account, the
band alignment would be rather flat or even a small cliff. This rela-
tively small negative value could be an explanation for the slightly
reduced VOC values (20–50 mV lower) of our In2S3-orIn2S3:
NaF-buffered CIGS cells compared to reference cells with a CdS
buffer.16,34 Considering the strongly lower CBM of In2O3 compared
