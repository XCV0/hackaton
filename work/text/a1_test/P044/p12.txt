Figure 5. Comparison of environmental impacts of one PTB7-Th:ZY-4Cl solar cell (functional unit:
0.04 cm2) under different electricity supply scenarios in Poland: (a) Global Warming Potential,
(b) Acidification Potential (AP), (c) Eutrophication Potential (EP), (d) Ozone Depletion Potential
(ODP), and (e) Fossil Fuel Depletion (FFD). Renewable energy substitution (hydro, wind, PV) substan-
tially reduces impacts compared to the Polish grid mix, while biogas and waste-to-energy introduce
trade-offs in AP, EP, and GWP.
