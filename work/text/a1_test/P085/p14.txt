QUANTIFICATION AND STATISTICAL ANALYSIS
In this study, statistical methods were primarily used to analyze the J–V performance parameters and their reproducibility, with a 
particular focus on the average values of these parameters. In addition, a basic quantitative and statistical analysis was conducted 
to evaluate the atomic ratios of Cs, Pb, and Br in the CsPbBr3 absorption layer, and the grain size distribution in ETL was also 
assessed.
