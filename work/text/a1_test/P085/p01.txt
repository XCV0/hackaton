Mg doped SnO2 electron transport layer enhances
planar all inorganic perovskite solar cells efficiency
Graphical abstract
Highlights
• Mg doping (3.0%) in SnO2 ETL improves PSC PCE to 7.17%
without HTL
• Enhanced charge transfer and reduced recombination at the
ETL/perovskite interface
• Devices fabricated with green solvents show improved
reproducibility and stability
• Achieved 32.6% efficiency gain and good ambient stability
without encapsulation
