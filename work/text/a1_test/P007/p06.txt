To further analyze the interactions between the perovskite ab -
sorber and the F- PEAI passivator, we studied structural and 
optical properties of the passivated films. No major differences 
were observed between the control and the passivated samples 
(Figures S9 and S10). Furthermore, the XRD studies, shown in 
Figure 3d, exhibited no signatures of any 2D or quasi-  2D per-
ovskite phase, revealing that the F- PEAI did not interact with 
the underlying perovskite film to form higher order perovskite 
phases as is commonly observed for organic–inorganic mixed 
cation perovskites upon ammonium ligand treatment [ 55]. To 
further rule out the possibility of nonconformal 2D or quasi-  2D 
perovskite layer formation upon F- PEAI treatment, we carried 
out hyperspectral imaging and microscopic PL measurements 
on the control and passivated samples. Figure  3e shows the PL 
spectra for the control and the passivated samples in the whole 
wavelength range of 360–800 nm, along with the microscopic PL 
images collected for both the samples in the lower wavelength 
range of 400–590 nm. The absence of any bright spots or PL 
peaks in the lower wavelength region, 360–600 nm, confirms 
the absence of any 2D perovskite phase formation, whether con-
formal or nonconformal. Instead, we believe that F- PEAI forms 
a molecular cation passivation layer on the perovskite layer as 
is reported in previous reports [ 4, 54] as well. This has been at -
tributed to strong resilience of Cs + towards cation exchange to 
form a 2D perovskite capping layer and is well documented for 
different cesium- based inorganic [4, 54, 56] and cesium- rich or-
ganic–inorganic perovskites [57]. Similar results were obtained 
with the hyperspectral PL measurements, the corresponding PL 
spectra, and images for both the control and passivated samples 
can be found in Figure S11.
