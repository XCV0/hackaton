3   |   Conclusion
In summary, the paper addresses the key challenges in fabricat-
ing and applying a high bandgap inorganic perovskite film in 
perovskite/perovskite/silicon triple- junction solar cells. It pres -
ents the first successful integration of a thermally evaporated 
all- inorganic CsPbI2Br perovskite top- cell in a monolithic triple- 
junction solar cell. The developed perovskite demonstrated ex -
cellent photostability with no signs of halide segregation under 
continuous illumination for 3 h, leading to a stabilized PCE  of 
21% for the triple-  junction solar cell. Although perovskite/per -
ovskite/silicon triple-  junction solar cells represent a complex 
and emerging technology, our work highlights promising solu -
tions for the robust processing of an efficient triple- junction solar 
cell. Evaporation technique not only overcomes the challenges 
associated with solvent selection but also ensures uniform film 
deposition over large areas with better thickness control, a key 
factor that can be leveraged for further optimization and scal -
ing of the triple-  junction devices. The as developed monolithic 
perovskite/perovskite/silicon triple-  junction solar cell with 
thermally evaporated CsPbI 2Br perovskite top cell not only en -
dured over 100 h of fixed voltage measurement under ambient 
conditions without encapsulation but also exhibited improved 
efficiency of ~22%. These results highlight the stability and re -
liability of these triple-  junction solar cells. Our future endeav -
ors will focus on improving the performance of the evaporated 
inorganic perovskite top cells by further optimizing the evapo -
ration process to enhance the device performance. This refine -
ment aims to enhance the crystallinity and bandgap of inorganic 
perovskite thin films at ambient temperature and optimize the 
selection of charge transport layers to further mitigate charge 
carrier selectivity losses. Additionally, for a truly scalable triple 
junction solar cell, it will be crucial to develop an evaporated 
middle cell and realize a fully evaporated perovskite/perovskite/
silicon triple junction.
