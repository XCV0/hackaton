4.4   |   PL Intensity
4.4.1   |   X- Ray Diffraction (XRD) Measurements
X- ray diffractograms were recorded using a Bruker D8 
Advance tool with Cu Kα source. Measurements were carried 
out in step size of 0.01°, and time per step was set at 0.5 s. The 
gracing incidence XRD was recorded at an angle of incidence 
of 5°.
4.4.2   |   SEM Measurements
Film morphology and cross-  section at different magnifications 
were obtained using a Schottky emission SEM (Zeiss, Auriga 60) 
at 5 kV.
4.4.3   |   PL Measurements
LuQY Pro Radiative efficiency meter model LP20- 32 instrument 
was used for PL and the implied open-  circuit voltage measure -
ments. The laser wavelength of 532 nm and spot size of 0.1 cm2 
were used to excite the perovskite samples at one sun equivalent 
intensity for this purpose.
4.4.4   |   Hyperspectral Imaging
Steady- state PL measurements were performed using a 
hyperspectral luminescence imaging system, which includes 
an optical microscope with a 100X objective and a 405-  nm 
laser at 1-  Sun equivalent intensity. Each scan took approxi -
mately 2 min. All samples were nonencapsulated and mea -
sured in air.
4.4.5   |   PL- Based iVOC Imaging
A measurement system developed at Fraunhofer ISE and built by 
Intego GmbH was used for the acquisition of PL images. A 450- 
nm laser was used to homogeneously illuminate the samples at 
1- Sun equivalent intensity. A 730-  nm LED was used to subcell 
selectively illuminate the middle cell in the triple-  junction de -
vice in Figure S14. More details on the measurement system and 
the iVOC imaging method can be found here [63].
4.4.6   |   Microscopic PL Measurements
A commercially available confocal Raman scanning microscope 
(Witec) was used to focus a laser beam (355 nm) for the 2D per-
ovskite measurements. The laser spot size was approximately 
1 μm for the used objective lens with 20X magnification. The 
emitted PL signal was spectrally resolved by coupling it via a 
dichroic mirror to a Czerny–Turner-  type spectrometer and de -
tecting the signal with a silicon charge-  coupled device (CCD) 
linear array detector.
4.4.7   |   UV–Vis Measurements
Reflectance and transmittance data in the wavelength range of 
300–1200 nm were collected using a lambda 950 spectrometer 
(PerkinElmer) tool. Absorptance for the perovskite films was 
estimated using the formula A = 1−R−T.
4.4.8   |   Transient PL Measurements
TrPLs were performed with a hybrid photomultiplier detector 
assembly from Picoquant and a time-  correlated single photon 
counting digitizer (Timeharp). A 515-  nm laser (Omicron) was 
used as a light source, and it was modulated digitally with a trig-
ger signal generated by an arbitrary wave-  form generator. The 
incident intensity was set to 51 mW/cm2. The on- time was set to 
500 ns, and the off-  time to 1.5 μs. The integration time was set 
to 300 s.
4.4.9   |   X- Ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy 
Measurements
XPS spectra were acquired on a SPECS Surface Nano Analysis 
GmbH, EnviroESCA X-  ray photoelectron spectrometer 
(PHOIBOS 150 NAP analyzer, 1D Delay Line Detector, mono-
chromatic Al Kα radiation at 1486.6 eV, incident angle 55°). 
Survey spectra were obtained with a pass energy of 100 eV, 
high- resolution spectra with 50 eV, and 20 accumulative 
scans. The signal of the I 3d5/2 electrons was used as binding 
energy reference at 618.50 eV. No charge control was required; 
the C 1s signals vary in the range of 284.2 and 284.3 eV. A 
depth profile was generated using an GCIB 10S ion gun from 
Ionoptika (10 kV, Ar+ ion sputtering, 6 × 6 mm2). The expected 
decomposition of perovskites, the occurrence of Pb(0) due to 
X- ray exposure and sputtering, was observed as reported in 
literature [48, 64, 65]. Therefore, a low sputter rate was chosen 
for gentle etching. The sputter limits were set to 720-  s etch-
ing time. The semiquantitative analysis was done considering 
temperature, mean free pathway, and the usage of relative 
sensitivity factors from CasaXPS. The elemental ratios were 
then calculated from the elemental distribution of the semi -
quantitative analysis.
