3. Results and Discussion 
Standard CdTe devices without GLAD CdTe interlayers are fabricated with the bulk 
CdTe deposited at HT and RT and having the device structure shown in Figure 1. HT bulk 
CdTe ﬁlms have pronounced crystallinity and more preferentially orientated crystallites 
with larger grain sizes when compared to RT bulk CdTe ﬁlms. The larger grains of HT 
bulk CdTe reduce grain boundaries and defect state density, leading to reduced photo-
generated carrier recombination. As expected, the device e ﬃciency of the standard HT 
CdTe cell without the interlayer is higher th an the standard RT CdTe cell without an in-
terlayer. Jsc, Voc, and FF increase from 21.36 mAcm−2, 0.767 V, and 58.62% for the RT base-
line device to 22.15 mAcm−2, 0.825 V, and 64.22% for the HT baseline device. The standard 
HT CdTe device shows a 3.2% absolute increase in eﬃciency when compared to the stand-
ard RT CdTe device, increasing from 9.59 to 12.76%.
