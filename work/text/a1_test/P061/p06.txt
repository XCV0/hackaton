Time-resolved photoluminescence (TRPL) was used to 
evaluate the non-radiative recombination of the Control and 
A&P perovskite films. As displayed in Figure 4a, the A&P 
sample exhibits an average recombination lifetime of 1.59 µ s, 
whereas the Control sample is 0.54 µ s, indicating the sup-
pressed non-radiative recombination and V loss. The space 
charge limited current (SCLC) measurement was also per -
formed to quantitatively analyze the trap density for the hole-
only devices, as illustrated in Figure  4b,c. According to the 
equation of N t  =  2εε0VTFL/qL2, the trap density (N t) can be 
calculated. Here q  is the elementary charge, L  is the thick-
ness of the perovskite film, ε  is the relative dielectric con-
stant of perovskite, and ε 0 is the vacuum permittivity. [29] The 
trap-filling limited voltage (V TFL), obtained from the dark J –V 
curves, is 0.90 and 0.33 V for the Control and A&P devices, 
and the corresponding N t is 2.7  × 1016 and 6.5  × 1015 cm−3 for 
the Control and A&P devices, respectively. The reduced trap 
density is well-matched with the longer carrier recombination, 
indicating the effects of synergetic regulation of oriented crys-
tallization and interfacial passivation for FPEAI on defect pas-
sivation can be well elaborated. Mott–Schottky plot was carried 
out to measure the build-in potential (V bi) by the capacitance-
voltage measurement. The relationship can be defined as 
1/C2 = 2(Vbi− V)/A2qεε0N,[30] where A  is the active region area 
of the device, q  is the unit charge, ε 0 and ε  are the vacuum 
and relative dielectric constant, respectively, N  is the carrier 
density, and V  is the applied bias. [31] As shown in Figure  4d, 
the Vbi is 0.95 and 1.04 V for Control and A&P device, respec-
tively. The enhanced V bi for the A&P device demonstrates that 
the improved passivation effect and the formed 2D/3D het-
erostructure contribute to a higher driving force for charge 
separation and promote the photogenerated carrier collec-
tion at the perovskite/HTL interface. [32] Therefore, the larger 
Vbi helps to obtain a higher V OC and remarkable photovoltaic 
performance with the synergetic strategy. The dependence
