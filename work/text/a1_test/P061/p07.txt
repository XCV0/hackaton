Good interfacial charge transfer requires a fine energy-
level alignment. We use ultraviolet photoelectron spectrom-
etry (UPS) measurement to determine the work function and 
energy band structure after the synergetic strategy. From the 
secondary electron cutoff spectrum (Figure  4g), we calculate 
the work functions are 4.7 and 4.5 eV for the Control and A&P 
films. There is no change in the Fermi edge region (Figure 4h). 
According to the UPS results, the position of the valence 
band (E V) for Control and A&P perovskite is 6.24 and 6.04 eV, 
respectively. Combined with the reported energy-level of the 
spiro-OMeTAD,[35] the corresponding energy-level alignment is 
illustrated in Figure 4i, in which the E V for the A&P perovskite 
can well-match the hole transport layer and facilitate efficient 
hole transport from the perovskite to the hole-transport-layer, 
which can effectively suppress the nonradiative recombination 
and reduce the Vloss.
To evaluate the effect of synergetic strategy on the photo-
voltaic performance, solar cells in the stack of ITO/SnO 2/
perovskite/spiro-OMeTAD/Ag were fabricated, Figure 5a and 
Table S2 (Supporting Information) depict the J–V curves and 
photovoltaic parameters of the champion devices. The Con-
trol device shows a PCE of 16.21% with a V OC of 1.18 V, J SC of 
18.76 mA cm −2, and FF of 72.74%. The Additive device delivers 
a PCE of 17.60% with a slightly improved V OC to 1.22 V, and an 
increased FF to 76.99%. The Passivation device has a PCE of 
17.88% with a high V OC of 1.29 V. Significantly, the V OC and FF 
for the A&P are improved to 1.30 V and 77.80%, and the small 
increase in J SC to 18.90 mA cm −2 is attributed to the minor 
shift of the UV–vis spectra (see in Figure S6 in the Supporting 
Information), which leads to an excellent PCE of 19.07%. In 
addition, we used the hysteresis index (HI) to evaluate the pas-
sivation effect and suppressed ion migration, which can be 
calculated as HI=(PCE RS−PCEFS)/PCERS.[36] From Figure  5b 
and Table 1, the A&P device delivers a HI of 0.9%, while the 
Control device is 2.2%, which demonstrate the reduced hyster -
esis by the synergetic strategy. The external quantum efficiency 
(EQE) was tested in Figure  5c, and the integrated J SC is 18.22 
and 18.03 mA cm −2, respectively. Moreover, Figure 5d exhibits 
a steady output of the A&P device under the maximum power 
point (MPP), a stabilized PCE of 18.56% is obtained. On a large-
area device of 1 cm 2 shown in Figure 5e, V OC of 1.31 V (1.29 V), 
JSC of 18.58 mA cm −2 (18.61 mA cm −2), FF of 61.54% (61.32%), 
and PCE of 15.01% (14.97%) can be obtained, indicating the 
potential for up-scaling PSCs of such synergetic strategy. This
