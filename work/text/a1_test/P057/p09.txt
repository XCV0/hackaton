paredintheair.Allchemicalsexceptthioureawereusedasreceivedwith-

0.5420 g Cu(oAC)2·H2O, 0.3700g SnCl2·2H2O, 0.3060 g ZnCl2,and

Fabrication of CZTSSe Absorber Films: The precursor ﬁlms were pre-
