A Related Work
A.1 RAG Hallucination
LLM hallucination refers to the phenomenon where
large language models generate plausible yet factu-
ally incorrect outputs, undermining their reliability
in real-world applications (Kaddour et al., 2023;
Kalai et al., 2025; Xu et al., 2025b). Retrieval-
augmented generation (RAG) alleviates this issue
by retrieving up-to-date and relevant external evi-
dence and conditioning generation on the retrieved
context (Gao et al., 2024; Fan et al., 2024; Zhang
et al., 2025; Guu et al., 2020; Lewis et al., 2021;
Santhanam et al., 2022; Varshney et al., 2023;
Mishra et al., 2024).
