B.6 Large Candidate-Pool Error Analysis
Before running the experiment, we manually in-
spected a stratified sample of 20 instances, all of
which satisfied the construction criteria for the
50-document candidate pools. For DeepSeek-V3,
Qwen-Max, and GLM-4-32B-0414, TrustRAG ob-
tains unsupported-answer rates of 0.00%, 2.00%,
and 9.00%, respectively, with corresponding valid-
response rates of 100.00%, 98.00%, and 91.00%.
Of the 11 unsupported answers produced by
TrustRAG, nine occur when GLM-4-32B-0414
fails to report an evidence conflict, while two occur
when Qwen-Max answers from distractor evidence
that passes the metadata predicates but is irrelevant
to the query. None of these cases involves access-
denied or explicitly superseded evidence, indicat-
ing that access and supersession filtering remain
stable in this setting, while the residual errors arise
primarily from model-dependent conflict reporting
and evidence selection.
