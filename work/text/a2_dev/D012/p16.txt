B Additional Experimental Results
This appendix provides additional evidence for the
experimental findings in Section 5. It includes sta-
tistical tests, supplementary baselines, per-model
ablations, boundary stress tests, utility diagnostics,
and human-audit details.
B.1 Statistical Tests
Table B1 provides full statistical details for the
target failure metrics reported in Section 5. For
each dataset, model, and failure type, we report
the Naive RAG failure rate, the TrustRAG failure
rate, bootstrap confidence intervals, absolute reduc-
tion in percentage points, and paired McNemar test
results. The large reductions and small McNemar
p-values on the main target failures indicate that the
observed improvements are not driven by a single
model or by sampling variance.
B.2 Supplementary Baselines
Table B2 reports additional governance and re-
trieval baselines. The governance rows extend the
source-anchored setting, while the retrieval rows
report BM25, dense retrieval, and prompt-based
retrieval pipelines by TEMPLATE slice. The slice-
level results show that RESTRICTED, FRESHNESS,
and CONFLICT primarily stress the Leakage, Stale
Citation, and Unsupported Answer metrics, respec-

B.3 Per-Model Ablations and
Prompt-Injection Stress
Table B3 expands the ablation and adversarial
stress-test results from Section 5.3. The ablation
rows show the per-model effects of removing ac-

reporting constraints. The prompt-injection rows
