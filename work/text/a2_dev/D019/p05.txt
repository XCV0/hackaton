3.2 Baselines
Since our primary focus is on exploring the distri-
bution gap between queries and different knowl-
edge bases, alleviating missing knowledge issues,
thereby improving the accuracy of open-domain
question answering and the completeness of re-
trieved contexts. We primarily consider the follow-
ing category of baselines: (1) DirectGen, whose
answer is directly generated by prompting the
reader LLM. (2) CoT (Wei et al., 2022) which
generate responses in a chain-of-thought paradigm
