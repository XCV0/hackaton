6 Conclusion
In this paper, we tackle key challenges in open-
book question answering by bolstering the robust-
ness of LLMs against distractors and enhancing
their reasoning capabilities. Our approach involved
fine-tuning LLMs with synthetic distractor data
and introducing a novel strategization method fo-
cused on dynamic CoT step generation. We showed
that leveraging the proposed iterative synthetic data
generation and refinement is an effective method
to produce train data with sufficient scale and com-
plexity for finetuning LLMs to boost their RAG
QA capability. Extensive evaluations across 12
public benchmarks demonstrated significant im-
provements in factual accuracy. These findings
highlight the potential of our approach to advance
the state-of-the-art, ultimately delivering a more
factual and reliable QA system.
Limitations
While our proposed method shows significant im-
provements in factuality for open-book question
answering, several limitations persist. First, the
reliance on synthetically generated distractor data
may not fully capture the complexity and variabil-
ity of real-world distractors, potentially limiting the
model’s robustness in diverse scenarios. Specifi-
cally, we acknowledge that the current method may
not fully capture certain real-world complexities
such as: HTML parsing artifacts/formatting incon-
sistencies, implicit contradictions requiring deep
domain knowledge, and perspective differences in
presenting similar events. Additionally, the use
of LLM-as-judge for factuality evaluation, both
in our training data pipeline and benchmarking,
presents its own challenges. For instance, it may
exhibit bias towards the presence or absence of ad-
ditional explanations, and its behavior can become
less predictable when there is a mismatch between
its internal parametric knowledge and the retrieved
references.
