6.2 Recommendations to Practitioners
Based on our observations, we make the following
recommendations for future practitioners consider-
ing using knowledge selectors to improve perfor-
mance in a real-world RAG scenario:
Benchmark the generation performance without
external knowledge, with all candidate knowledge,
and if possible, with gold knowledge only. The “no
knowledge” setting measures the generator’s base
performance, “full knowledge” serves as a strong
baseline corresponding to knowledge retrieval with
perfect recall, and “gold knowledge” gives the up-
per bound of RAG performance with perfect knowl-
edge selection. The gap between “full knowledge"
and “gold knowledge" is the potential performance
gain brought by adding a knowledge selector.
To improve RAG performance, increasing the
knowledge recall score is the most effective strat-
egy. Thus, for modern LLM generators, improving
the knowledge retriever’s recall is key. In prac-
