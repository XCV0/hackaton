Evaluation protocol. We target entities with gen-
uine opinion diversity by computing sentiment en-
tropy H(e) and selecting those with H(e) ≥ 0.6
ensuring both positive and negative sentiments are
represented (4 entities for forums, 15 for Opin-
ioBank). For each entity, we generate natural lan-
guage questions across multiple types designed
to probe different facets of opinion. This yielded
48 questions for forums and 57 for OpinioBank.
Each question is submitted to both KB configu-
rations. Then, we retrieve the top-k documents
and generate responses using the retrieved context.
Retrieved documents are matched back to opinion
metadata to compute diversity metrics.
Metrics. We design proxy metrics aligned with
the theoretical objectives (Equation 8).
For coverage: (1) Sentiment-Intensity σ: com-
bines sentiment polarity and intensity into a sin-
gle numeric score (Positive+High = +30, Posi-
tive+Medium = +20, Positive+Low = +10, Neu-
tral = 0, Negative+Low = −10, Negative+Medium
= −20, Negative+High = −30); we report the
standard deviation across retrieved documents,
where higher σ indicates broader coverage of the
sentiment-intensity space. (2) Business Impact
σ: maps impact levels to numeric values (High
= 30, Medium = 20, Low = 10); higher σ indi-
cates retrieval of both high-impact and low-impact
perspectives. (3) Entity Match Rate: fraction of
retrieved documents that mention the queried entity,
measuring retrieval precision for opinion-relevant
content. (4) Emotional Markers: count of distinct
emotional markers (e.g., Frustration, Hope, Resig-
nation) across retrieved documents. (5) Empirical
W1: Wasserstein distance between corpus-level
and retrieved sentiment distributions.
For fidelity: human evaluation via blind pairwise
comparison on the forums dataset (5 annotators,
48 pairs).
For fairness: Demographic Coverage, the frac-
tion of categories represented in retrieved docu-
ments for each author attribute: Revenue Band
(business size), Tenure (experience levels), and Ev-
idence Type (personal, anecdotal, data-driven).

5.2 Results
Coverage. Table 2 presents the core comparison
at k=20 with hybrid retrieval on the forums dataset.
Opinion enrichment significantly improves all cov-
erage metrics: +42.7% entity match rate, +26.8%
sentiment-intensity σ, +14.5% business impact σ,
and +23.6% emotional markers (all p<0.05).
To directly validate the connection to our theoret-
ical framework, we compute empirical W1 between
corpus-level and retrieved sentiment distributions
(Table 3). The enriched KB is consistently 18-48%
closer to corpus-level sentiment truth across both
domains.
Fidelity. Human annotators confirmed that
opinion-enriched retrieval produces responses
that better communicate opinion diversity. Of
48 question-response pairs, annotators preferred
Opinion-Enriched KB responses 79.2% of the time
(95% CI: [65.0%, 89.5%], p<0.001). Appendix D
has more details about the annotation study. Ap-
pendix F provides a qualitative example showing
how enriched responses capture sentiment distri-
butions and constructive suggestions missing from
baseline responses.
