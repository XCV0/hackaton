the Labeled Property Graph (LPG). The schema encodes the graph architecture, including all node labels, properties,
and both incoming and outgoing edge types for each node. This structural information enables the text2cypher model to
understand how to traverse the graph, which relationships connect nodes, and what properties and labels are available
