framework for evaluating multimodal RAG.
4 MIRAGE
MIRAGE (Figure 2) is a subclaim-centric evalua-
tion framework that evaluates two dimensions of
predictions: the presented information and the use
of citations. INFOF1 measures both the proportion
of predicted subclaims that are factual (precision)
as well as the proportion of a set of reference sub-
claims that are supported by the predicted text (re-
call). CITEF1 measures whether subclaims are
supported by their cited sources (precision) and
whether reference subclaims attested by the predic-
tion are attributed to a source that supports them (re-
call). Appendix B provides further variants of MI-
RAGE for single-source inference and subclaim
and source importance weighting.
