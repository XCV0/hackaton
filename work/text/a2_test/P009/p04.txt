3.2. Personalized-Topic RAG (PT-RAG)
PT-RAG is designed to retrieve a new and inter-
esting topic for a user by leveraging LLM inference
to identify a topic candidate that are absent from
a user’s inferred knowledge (derived from previ-
ously disclosed information). Because supplying
a full dump of a user’s knowledge or the entire
candidate pool is impractical given input-length
and scale constraints, PT-RAG groups both user
knowledge and candidates into topic categories
(e.g., music, travel) and performs category-guided
retrieval, providing the LLM only with category-
relevant summaries and candidates.

3.2.1. Terminologies and Task Deﬁnition
Terminologies: Table 1 shows the notations used

candidate set T = {tj }J j=1, referring to the topic

In and Kn are estimated from the given history of
