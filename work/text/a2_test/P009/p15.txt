B. Example of Conversation in the
Human Evaluation
An example conversation demonstrating the ﬁnd-
ings described in Section 4.2.4 is provided in Table
5. To improve readability, we present summarized
versions of the conversations rather than their full
content. In the passive scenario, where the par-
ticipant was required to initiate every conversa-
tion, they ran out of topics by the second interac-
tion, resulting in exchanges limited to greetings. In
the experiment shown in the table, the participant
never chose the passive chatbot, likely due to the
inconvenience of choosing. The proactive chat-
bot asked questions about disclosed information,
clearly demonstrating memory of past conversa-
tions. However, as seen in the third conversation,
it tended to produce similar responses when no
new information was disclosed. In contrast, Topic-
Initiator introduced new topics not covered in pre-
vious interactions, varying the topics each time.
When comparing Topic-Initiator with the proactive
chatbot, the participant chose the proactive chat-
bot for the ﬁrst two conversations but switched to
Topic-Initiator from the third conversation onward.
