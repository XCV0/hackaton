Reasoning: Topic-Initiator does not currently pro-
vide explicit reasoning for why particular topics
are chosen. As a result, users may perceive the
selected topics as unrelated to past interactions,
which could reduce their willingness to continue
the conversation. Incorporating transparent rea-
soning that links topic selection to prior exchanges
may help increase user motivation to engage.
Topic Shift: At present, Topic-Initiator acts proac-
tively only at the beginning of a conversation and
cannot actively shift topics during ongoing inter-
actions. While it is technically feasible to perform
topic transitions by executing PT-RAG dynamically
based on the conversation ﬂow and leveraging its
outputs, determining the appropriate timing and
strategy for such transitions remains a challeng-
ing open problem (Xie et al., 2021; Hwang et al.,
2024).
Ethical Statement
Preventing malicious use: We recognize the po-
tential risks of malicious use of our system, such
as the extraction of personal data. To mitigate
these risks, the chatbot is designed to anonymize
any sensitive personal information from its utter-
ances.
Informed consent: All participants involved in
the human evaluation were fully informed about
the study’s purpose, procedures, potential risks,
and beneﬁts, and provided written consent. Par-
ticipants were also clearly informed that they
could withdraw from the study at any time without
penalty.
