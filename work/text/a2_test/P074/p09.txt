Limitations
Despite its strong performance, our method still
has two main limitations. First, the overall upper
bound is constrained by the quality of visual coarse-
grained retrieval. To balance efficiency and perfor-
mance, we do not substantially expand the initial re-
trieval scope, which leaves room for improvement
in later stages. Second, hard-negative sampling
based on decoding-rank perturbation is computa-
tionally expensive because it relies on LLM infer-
ence, making it difficult to scale to the full training
corpus. Moreover, the current sampling strategy is
static. Future work could explore dynamic hard-
negative selection based on generator feedback, for
example through reinforcement learning.
