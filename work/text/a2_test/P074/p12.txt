Generation Stage: For the LLaVA training phase,
we randomly selected 100,000 samples from each
of the two datasets to serve as training data. We
employed LoRA for fine-tuning the LLM, with the
following parameter settings: r = 128 and alpha =
256. The learning rate was set to 2e-5, the batch
size to 16, and the total number of epochs to 1.
