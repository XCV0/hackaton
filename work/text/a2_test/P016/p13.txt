G Translation-Following
We instruct Llama-3-8b using instruction set
composed from Instruction: ‘Translate
the following text from LX to
English’, Input: ‘Sentence in LX ’.
and Output: ‘Sentence in English’.,
as in (Ranaldi et al., 2024). We used
news_commentary (Tiedemann, 2012) by
selecting En-X translations as detailed in Table
9. We randomly extracted 2k demonstrations for
the available languages in the open repo (link1,
link2). We tune the model for three epochs with
a batch size of 32 and a learning rate equal to
1e-5 with a 0.001 weight decay. We use the
cosine learning rate scheduler with a warmup
ratio of 0.03. We conducted our experiments
on a workstation with four Nvidia RTX A6000
48VRAM for approximately 14 GPU/h.
