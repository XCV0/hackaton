future, we plan to conduct joint training of reranker
and rewrite models, which may yield substantial
benefits for RAG.
Limitations
Although our experiments employ a general
reranker as the source of feedback signals, there
are still some limitations. (1) The Lack of Cross-
Domain Validation. As constrained by the lack
of domain-specific data, we lack the validation of
separately trained rerankers on datasets pertinent
to a specific domain. (2) Reliance on the Effec-
tiveness of Rewriting as a Bottleneck. Although
we can achieve some improvements by using pub-
licly available rerankers, this enhancement may be
limited by the capability of the reranker.
