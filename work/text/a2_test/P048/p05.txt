Entities in Vt are later served as graph nodes, hence we denote
them as entity nodes. For our entity extraction procedure, the
entity boundary b and entity extraction threshold τ are con-
sidered as hyper-parameters, we further analyze their setting
in Section IV-D.
After entity extraction, we extract relations from text chunks
t ∈ Tc based on its extracted entities Vt. Align with other
Graph-based RAG models, we also apply LLM at this step:
Et = LLM(Vt, t, pre), (4)
where Vt denotes the extracted entities of text chunk t, and in
the relation extraction prompt pre, we ask the LLM to detect
relations between entities in Vt w.r.t. text chunk t. Although
we also apply LLM for relation extraction, we input less
noise in this step, mitigating the error propagation, as our
statistics-based entity extraction avoids LLM hallucination,
hence resulting in a more comprehensive and less noisy Vt
as the Equation (4) input.
After relation extraction, we add text chunks in Tc to V as
passage nodes, and add passage edges e(v,t) between entities
contained within the text chunks to E, the respective relation
of these edges is namely Contains. By introducing passage
nodes, we integrate contextual information into the constructed
KG, and the entity-passage edges can capture the inter-text
correlations, make our model able to generate reasoning paths
across different text chunks [29].

C. Step 2: Graph Retrieval
In this subsection, we introduce the graph retrieval proce-
dure of AGRAG. Given the KG KG(V, E) constructed at the
Step 1, we first map the query q to KG and obtain raw mapped
triplet facts Fraw. To achieve this, we encode each triplet fact
in KG f ∈ FKG and the query q into vector representations
q and f with encoder M: q ← M(q), then select facts with
top-k dense semantic mapping score MS to query q based on
their cosine similarity:
MS(q, f) = cosine sim(q, f), (7)
Fraw = {f ∈ FKG | f ∈ arg ⊤ka (M S(q, f))} , (8)
where ka is a hyperparameter that controls the number of raw
mapped triples extracted, and f are obtained through encoding
the text concatenation of entities and relation of the triplet fact.
Further, as the triplets in KG are extracted in a query
unaware manner, query irrelevant triplets can exist in the raw
mapped triplet facts Fraw, hence we apply LLM-based filter
on Fraw to generate the mapped triplet facts F, to reduce the
query irrelevant noise:
F = LLM(Fraw, q, ptf), (9)
where ptf denotes the LLM prompt for triple filtering.
