E RAGAS Faithfulness: Categorization
Note
The RAGAS Faithfulness baseline uses the
FaithfulnesswithHHEM variant. Although it in-
vokes an LLM to decompose claims into sub-
statements, the final faithfulness judgment relies on
an NLI model (HHEM) scoring each claim against
the retrieved context, making it conceptually closer
to an NLI-based approach. Furthermore, the faith-
fulness accuracy is not sensitive to which LLM
performs the decomposition step. For these rea-
sons, RAGAS Faithfulness is categorized as an
NLI method in all result tables and excluded from
parameter count discussions.
F HotPotQA-Derived: Quality Control
Details
Initial Quality Control. During data generation,
we conducted stratified sampling at approximately
1:500. The concise answer styles of HotPotQA
make it nearly impossible for generated halluci-
nated answers to be factually incorrect when fol-
lowing this style.
Comprehensive Quality Validation. LLMs
flagged 1,173 potentially problematic samples out
of 72,391. Manual secondary verification on all
flagged samples confirmed no factual generation
errors.
