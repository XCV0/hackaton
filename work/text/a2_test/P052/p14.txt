D Tool-Using LLM Agent Baseline
The agent receives the same corpus schema and
metadata as the retrieval baselines and can use lexi-
cal search, authority-field search, validity filtering,
and passage inspection. It searches the corpus di-
rectly rather than reranking AuthorityFirst-RAG or
BM25 outputs, and receives no oracle evidence
or gold authority metadata from the QA records.
Table 14 reports its results on the balanced subset.
For the tool-using LLM agent, the gold passage
was retrieved in 25.0% of instances and entered the
final candidate set in 23.0% of instances. Condi-
tional accuracy when the gold passage was present
in the final candidate set was 65.2%.
