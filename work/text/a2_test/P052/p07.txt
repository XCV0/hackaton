6.3 Better evidence changes the structure of
generation errors
We classify erroneous generations using checklist-
based manual review with heuristic consistency
checks; the plotting script tabulates category shares.
Rule application errors occur when the evidence
is correct or near-correct but the model misapplies
a comparator, deadline, interval, or numeric condi-
tion, or contradicts its own reasoning. Semantic
grounding errors occur when the model fails to
use the applicable legal rule, often because the ev-
idence is mismatched or the model latches onto
non-applicable text. Output stability errors oc-
cur when the model fails to produce a parseable
yes/no answer. Appendix B gives fine-grained defi-
nitions and counts. Appendix B also reports model-
wise error composition, showing that the shift from
semantic-grounding to rule-application errors is not
driven by a single generator.
Figure 3 shows the dual bottleneck at the error
level. Under GoldOracle and AuthorityFirst-RAG
evidence, most errors are rule-application er-
rors: 91.1% and 83.0%, respectively. Un-
der BM25-date-filter, semantic-grounding er-
rors become dominant at 60.3%. Under
citation-hybrid date filter, semantic-grounding er-
rors are 40.9% and output-stability errors rise to
9.9%. Better evidence therefore changes the struc-
ture of failure rather than eliminating failure.
The shift is important. Weak evidence pushes
models toward grounding mistakes: they use the
wrong version, copy non-applicable text, or miss
the controlling rule. Strong evidence leaves failures
concentrated in legal rule application, so retrieval
quality changes the likely error type.
6.4 Upper-bound comparator failures persist
under gold evidence
A prominent source of rule-application error is
upper-bound comparator failure. Legal expressions
such as not more than, shall not exceed, maximum
penalty, capped at, and within X days define upper-
bound constraints. The fact must be less than or
equal to the threshold, or must occur before the
deadline. Models often reverse the direction and
treat the threshold as a lower bound. This can turn
a compliant fact into a violation, or an untimely
filing into a timely one.
Figure 4 shows that this failure persists under
GoldOracle evidence. The model is not simply
missing the relevant passage; it is misreading the le-
gal comparison after the correct passage is supplied.
