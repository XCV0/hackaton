2.3 Why citation match is insufficient
Citation is an important legal signal, but it is not
equivalent to law in force. The same citation can
refer to multiple historical versions of the same sec-
tion, and legal corpora often contain both obsolete
and current text. If a question asks about conduct
in an earlier period, the current version may be the
easiest passage to retrieve while being legally in-
applicable. Conversely, a superseded version may
govern an older fact pattern even though it is not
the current rule. The user’s factual date determines
which version is authoritative.
2.4 Scope
LawInForce-eCFR focuses on a foundational case:
real legal amendments that change numeric thresh-
olds and induce answer flips. Examples include
deadlines, monetary caps, percentages, employee
counts, penalties, ages, and frequencies. More com-
plex issues such as retroactivity, transition clauses,
tolling, and multi-rule statutory interpretation are
left outside the benchmark. This restricted scope
is intentional because it yields a controlled setting
where wrong-version retrieval and numeric rule
application can be diagnosed cleanly.

LawInForce-eCFR is built from versioned U.S. fed-

to cover all jurisdictions or legal sources; it tar-
gets the retrieval problem created when a corpus

summarizes the benchmark.

3.2 Numeric threshold alignment
For each candidate pair, we align old and new
rule sentences, extract the changed numeric val-
ues and units, and infer the comparator relation
expressed by the rule. The comparator may be an
upper bound, lower bound, deadline, range, cap,
minimum, maximum, or frequency constraint. De-
terministic checks require that the relevant values
appear in the legal text, that the units are compat-
ible, and that the old and new sentences express
the same underlying rule apart from the changed
threshold.
Numeric threshold changes are useful for diag-
nosis because they allow answer flips to be con-
structed without inventing a new legal issue. If the
old threshold is 60 days and the new threshold is
90 days, a fact value of 75 days lies between the
thresholds. The same fact pattern therefore receives
opposite yes/no answers depending on which ver-
sion is legally applicable. The benchmark uses
these controlled facts to make wrong-version re-
trieval observable.
