Limitations
In this paper, we introduce T-RAG, a graph-based
method for cross-table retrieval. There remain op-
portunities for further improvement. For instance,
the graph retrieval process could be enhanced by
incorporating more expressive graph neural net-
works (GNNs), such as Graph Attention Networks
(GAT) (Veliˇckovi´c et al., 2017; Zhou et al., 2020).
Additionally, incorporating downstream LLM fine-
tuning techniques (Hu et al., 2022; Li et al., 2023)
may further enhance end-to-end performance by
better aligning the reasoning model with the re-
trieved table contexts.
Ethics Statement
The proposed method and benchmark in this paper
has been carefully reviewed to ensure that all data
sources comply with applicable legal and ethical
standards. We have only used data that are free
from personally identifiable information (PII) and
sensitive attributes and have not modified or added
any data that could compromise the anonymity or
privacy of individuals.
