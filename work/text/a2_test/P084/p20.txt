A.12 Output Observation
Table 23 illustrates a qualitative comparison of the
retrieved contexts and generated responses across
different RAG methods. In this example, the user
expresses feelings of grief and isolation after at-
tempting to connect with their deceased mother
through a gift. The baseline models (gpt-embed-
3-large, Light-RAG, and RAG-Anything) retrieve
contexts that are broadly related to grief but do not
fully capture the specific nuances of the situation,
occasionally introducing unrelated themes such as
work stress or unresolved guilt. As a result, their
generated responses tend to offer general support.
In contrast, the proposed method utilizes Appraisal
and CBT projections to model the user’s underlying
emotional state and the symbolic meaning of the
gift. This cognitive context enables the system to
retrieve evidence that closely matches the reference
answer. Consequently, the generated response is
more contextually aligned, directly addressing the
user’s specific experience and providing suitable
therapeutic guidance.
