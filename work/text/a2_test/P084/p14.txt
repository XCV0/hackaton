ity levels, while MentalChat is more concentrated

that both datasets include a quite large propor-

57.9% of CounselChat.

A.2 Significance Testing
In the paper, “p ≤ 0.05 with 95% CI” refers
to paired two-sided t-tests on per-sample differ-
ences: for Recall@K we test the per-query bi-
nary hit/miss difference between two systems; for
BLEU/ROUGE/BERTScore we test the per-sample
score difference; for LM judge and human ratings
we test the per-sample rater-averaged scores. For
human evaluation (N =100, 5 raters), inter-rater
reliability is reported as Fleiss’ κ in Section 5.2.2.
