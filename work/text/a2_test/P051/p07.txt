5.4. Test generation Setup
In our evaluation, we automatically generated 30 PDF
files and we manually selected 30 manually selected PDF
files from Kaggle [21], i.e., we employed a total of 60 PDF
files as context. For each of the documents, 50 different
test inputs were generated by each of the techniques, i.e.,
RAG-TESTER and the baseline. That is, RAG-TESTER and
its baseline version generated 3,000 test cases each.
The rationale for selecting the 30 PDFs from Kaggle
were the following: Each document had to be at least 5
pages long, and the selection as a whole needed to cover a
diverse range of formats, including documents with tables
and images as well as simpler text-only files. Documents
containing headers, footers and graphical elements were
prioritized, in addition to those with varied text styles and
font sizes. This diversity was intentional, as testing across
differentdocumentstructuresgivesamorethoroughpicture
of how well the models handle real-world PDFs.
