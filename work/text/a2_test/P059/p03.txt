Grounded generation and attribution.
Grounded-generation methods instruct the
LLM to base its response on retrieved context
with inline citations: ALCE (Gao et al., 2023a)
and Self-RAG (Asai et al., 2024) provide such
protocols, and self-correcting methods such
as Self-Correcting RAG (Xu et al., 2026) use
natural language inference (NLI)-guided test-time
search to improve grounding at generation time.
Attribution benchmarks evaluate whether answers
are supported by cited sources, including Attribut-
edQA (Bohnet et al., 2022), ExpertQA (Malaviya
et al., 2024), FactScore (Min et al., 2023), and the
evaluation framework of Adlakha et al. (2024);
faithfulness has been framed as an explanation that
reflects what a model actually used (Jacovi and
Goldberg, 2020).
Context-intervention probes and causal formal-
ism. ContextCite formalizes context-attribution
via binary mask ablations (Cohen-Wang et al.,
2024), and entity-substitution and counter-memory
studies probe knowledge conflicts via context ed-
its (Longpre et al., 2021; Xie et al., 2024); redun-
dant evidence further motivates cluster-level rather
than single-document deletion (Chen et al., 2024;
Saha Roy et al., 2025). Causal and interventional
formalisms underpin the perturbation-and-effect
discipline that COUNTERMEM adopts (Pearl, 2009;
Feder et al., 2022). COUNTERMEM unifies single-
mask deletion and counterfactual-addition interven-
tions at three explicit pipeline points (post-retrieval,
corpus-addition, corpus-deletion), reads a stage-
localizable trace off the same intervention from
the generations the behavioral test already issues,
and complements rather than replaces aggregate
diagnostics like RAGAS and RAGChecker.
3 Counterfactual Evaluation of
Non-Parametric Memory Use
COUNTERMEM evaluates whether a fixed RAG
configuration’s outputs are behaviorally bound to
its retrieved non-parametric memory. It intervenes
on the retrieved state, regenerates with the same
generator and prompt, and measures the behavioral
transition under the evidence-bound criteria of Sec-
tion 1.
