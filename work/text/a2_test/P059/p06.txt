5 Results
We organize the results around three questions:
(1) does each COUNTERMEM signal flag failure
modes that observational baselines miss (§5.1)?
(2) does the same intervention trace localize the
pipeline stage to repair (§5.2)? (3) what behav-
ioral failures does COUNTERMEM reveal among
answers that aggregate diagnostics already approve
(§5.3)? and (4) do these findings survive a change
of generator family, judge family, and detector
(§5.4)? Construct-validity, end-to-end corpus dele-
tion, distribution-shift transfer (Appendix B), and
robustness checks are deferred to the appendix. The
RAG configurations throughout are evaluation fix-
tures, not proposed systems.
5.1 Failure-mode Coverage on RAGBench-27
COUNTERMEM flags update-inertia and
contradiction-ignorance failures that obser-
vational baselines rarely co-flag. We sweep
27 RAGBench configurations (3 datasets × 3
retrievers × k ∈ {2, 4, 8}, n=150 queries per
cell) and ask, for each evidence-bound failure
mode, how many configurations COUNTERMEM
flags as failing and how many of those are also
flagged by RAGAS-faithfulness or RAGChecker
hallucination at standard thresholds (Table 2).
No-evidence non-abstention is partially visi-
ble to observational signals. Of the 18 configu-
rations that fail to abstain reliably under Delete-
All, RAGAS-faithfulness co-flags 6 (33%) and
RAGChecker hallucination co-flags 9 (50%) on
the realized base answer. Configurations that rarely
abstain when context is removed tend also to have
lower realized-trace support, so faithfulness and
hallucination scores partially correlate with absten-
tion behavior, but neither signal sees the counterfac-
tual directly. The persistence-substantive flag DAV
reported in the protocol gives a stricter view tied to
the answer text rather than to abstention markers.
Update inertia and contradiction ignorance
are rarely co-flagged by observational baselines.
Of the 7 configurations that fail update propaga-
tion and the 12 that fail conflict acknowledgment,
RAGAS-faithfulness co-flags 0 on either axis and
RAGChecker hallucination co-flags 0 and 1 re-
spectively. This is structural: faithfulness and
hallucination score the realized (q, c, a) trace, but
update-following and conflict-acknowledgment are
properties of the answer under counterfactual ev-
idence. The clearest demonstration of the gap:
among the 31 same-dataset configuration pairs
whose RAGAS-faithfulness scores match within
±0.01, the maximum within-pair behavioral dif-
ference is 0.31 in update following and 0.29 in
conflict acknowledgment (median 0.11 and 0.13).
The pairing threshold is not doing the work: at
±0.005 (20 pairs) the maximum gaps are 0.27 and
0.17, at ±0.02 (53 pairs) they are 0.31 and 0.29,
with medians between 0.08 and 0.13 throughout
(Table 17).
