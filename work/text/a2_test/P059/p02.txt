2 Related Work
Observational and diagnostic RAG evaluation.
Reference-free evaluators score the realized query–
context–answer trace. RAGAS (Es et al., 2024)
and ARES (Saad-Falcon et al., 2024) report faith-
fulness, relevance, and context-precision; RAG-
Bench aggregates task-specific judge protocols
(Friel et al., 2024); recent surveys study judge relia-
bility and metric design (Liu et al., 2025; Ngo et al.,
2024; Wang et al., 2024). Fine-grained diagnos-
tic frameworks decompose answer quality further:
RAGChecker (Ru et al., 2024) uses claim-level en-
tailment for retriever and generator metrics, CoFE-
RAG (Liu et al., 2024a) evaluates chunking and
multi-stage diagnostics, RAGTrace (Cheng et al.,
2025) analyzes retrieval–generation interactions,
and RAGVUE (Murugaraj et al., 2026) decom-
poses reference-free evaluation with structured ex-
planations. Metamorphic-testing approaches such
as MetaRAG (Sok et al., 2025) probe hallucina-
tions by perturbing factoids in the answer and re-
checking entailment against the retrieved context.
