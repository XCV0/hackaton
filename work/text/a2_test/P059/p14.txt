H Detector Validation Protocol
To validate the two detectors behind the head-
line rates of §5.3, we drew a stratified sample
from the Claude-Haiku coherent-intervention run:
50 update-following decisions and 50 conflict-
acknowledgment decisions, spread evenly over the
six datasets (seed fixed). Two authors labeled the
sample independently through a web interface that
showed the question, the reference answer, the
injected counterfactual claim, and the system’s
answer—exactly the judge’s input—and offered
the judge’s four-label taxonomy; the judge’s la-
bel was withheld. Table 14 reports inter-annotator
agreement, detector agreement with each annotator,
precision and recall on the reported failure class,
and detector agreement on the items where the an-
notators concur. The disagreements between anno-
tators concentrate on the adopted/mixed boundary
of update following (an answer that states the new
value while hedging toward the old one), which is
why the four-way κ is low there while the binary
“ignored” decision, the one the paper reports, is
stable.
I Update vs. Contradiction Framing
The two counterfactual-addition probes share the
generator prompt (“Answer ONLY using the pro-
vided context. If context is insufficient or contradic-
tory, say you don’t know.”) and differ only in the
appended passage. Both passages are synthesized
by Claude-Haiku from the question and the refer-
ence answer and assert a specific alternative an-
swer of the same factual type. The update passage
is written to supersede: it presents the alternative
as the new correct answer (“Recent sources have
established that . . . ”). The contradiction passage
is written to compete: it asserts the alternative as
fact, with no temporal cue, so that the context now
carries two incompatible claims of equal standing.
The expected behavior therefore differs: under an
update, an evidence-bound system should adopt
the new value (adopted; failure is ignored, with
mixed and abstain as intermediate labels); under a
contradiction, it should surface the disagreement
(conflict_ack; failure is silent_pick of the injected
value or confident_match to the original, with ab-
stain as the cautious label). The judge receives the
question, the reference answer, the injected value,
and the system answer, and returns exactly one of
the four labels.
