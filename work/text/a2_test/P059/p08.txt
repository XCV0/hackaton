The failure rate varies by domain. TechQA
approved answers exhibit both failures on the ma-
jority (67.7% [50.1, 81.4], n=31); EManual is

most half the time (49.4%). The cross-domain
gradient indicates that compatibility-with-context
(RAGChecker’s signal) and behavioral following-
of-context (COUNTERMEM’s signal) are correlated
but not equivalent.
Worked example. On the HotpotQA query
“What city is the women’s Vans Triple Crown of Surf-
ing event hosted at?” the baseline answers Hon-
olulu and RAGChecker reports rch_hall = 0.000.
After appending a coherent update specifying Sun-
set Beach, the model continues to answer Hon-
olulu (update ignored). After appending a coherent
contradiction stating Sunset Beach, the model still
answers Honolulu with no conflict marker (silent
confident match). The aggregate diagnostic has no
signal here; the intervention does.
The pattern reproduces on natural conflicts
and on a human-grounded endpoint. On 100
DelucionQA naturally-conflicting passages, silent-
pick failures rise from 4% at baseline to 24% un-
der one-sided context removal (Appendix E). On
the ExpertQA human-support endpoint, adding the
COUNTERMEM profile to RAGAS lifts ROC-AUC
under both judges (+0.150 under a weak GPT-4o-
mini judge, +0.066 under a strong Claude-Sonnet
judge; both p<0.001, Table 10). The lift is carried
primarily by 1 − DAP; CNS independently corre-
lates with the human label (Spearman ρ ≈ 0.2) but
adds little incremental signal on top of 1 − DAP
in the ensemble, and NR is uncorrelated with the
human-support endpoint as expected, since clause
(5) and clauses (1)–(2) target different evidence-
bound criteria (Appendix D).
End-to-end corpus deletion confirms the evi-
dence boundary, and exposes a residual-corpus
regime. Removing gold-supporting chunks from
the index and rerunning retrieval moves absten-
tion from baseline to 0.99 on HotpotQA and 1.00
on PubMedQA: the system follows the evidence
boundary end-to-end. On TechQA, abstention
only moves 0.51 → 0.57 because large technical-
manual gold passages leave many related residual
chunks in the corpus; the probe makes that corpus
property operationally testable (Appendix F).
