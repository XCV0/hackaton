6 Conclusion
For RAG systems that promise to answer from
retrieved evidence, observational diagnostics ask
the wrong question. Whether an answer is con-
sistent with the realized context tells us nothing
about whether the system would still produce that
answer if the evidence were removed, updated, or
contradicted. COUNTERMEM operationalizes the
counterfactual: it intervenes on the retrieved state,
regenerates with the same generator and prompt,
and measures the behavioral transition.
The protocol exposes failures invisible to aggre-
gate scoring. On RAGBench’s 27-configuration
sweep, same-dataset matched-faithfulness pairs dif-
fer by up to 0.31 in update following and 0.29
in conflict acknowledgment (n=31 matched-faith
pairs). On six datasets, 38.8% [34.4, 43.4] of
RAGChecker-approved answers silently ignore a
coherent retrieved update and 55.6% [51.0, 60.1]
miss a coherent contradiction (Wilson 95% CIs
over the judged-valid subset, n=446). The same
intervention trace localizes the pipeline stage to
repair: matched repairs produce large gains on
retrieval- and rerank-broken fixtures (+0.40 and
+0.54 reactive rate), a significant gain on the nat-
ural HotpotQA generator class (+0.37 vs. naive
pool/k expansion), and a smaller non-significant
gain on the engineered generator fixture. The fail-
ure rates persist when the generator is replaced by
GPT-5.4-mini or Qwen3-8B and when the judge
is replaced by an out-of-family model, and the de-
tectors behind them agree with blind human labels
as closely as two annotators agree with each other.
This lets practitioners select, debug, and audit RAG
configurations against requirements that aggregate
evaluators cannot enforce alone.
Limitations
The protocol depends on the chosen interventions
and detectors. Update and contradiction pay-
loads are LLM-synthesized rather than human-
curated. A per-row Claude-Sonnet audit of all
593 coherence-conditioned contradictions finds
93.6% well-formed; a separate stratified audit of
the broader 27-configuration sweep (n=50 per
class; Appendix J) finds Delete-All, update, and
contradiction payloads 100%, 96%, and 82% well-
formed—the lower contradiction figure reflects the
sweep’s one-line templated payloads, not the co-
herent passages behind the headline rates, which
are computed on the judged-valid subset. Holding
the payloads fixed across generators (§5.4) isolates
dependence on the generator under test but does
not test the payload synthesizer: every payload is
written by Claude-Haiku. The behavioral detec-
tors are validated against blind human labels on
n=100 items (Appendix H); the update-following
taxonomy is subjective at the adopted/mixed bound-
ary. Answer-similarity detectors can miss small
but consequential factual edits, and cluster-deletion
granularity defines the unit of evidence removal.
Sample sizes vary by cell: pooled headline rates
are computed over 446 RAGChecker-approved an-
swers with valid contradictions, but per-dataset
cells (especially TechQA, n=31) carry wider inter-
vals (roughly ±16 points for TechQA). COUNTER-
MEM is behavioral and pipeline-level: it observes
whether outputs change under counterfactual re-
trieved states and at which stage an intervention
was lost, not which internal computation produced
