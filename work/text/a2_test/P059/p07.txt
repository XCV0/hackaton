5.3 Coherent-intervention Failures Among
RAGChecker-approved Answers
Behavioral failures are common among
RAGChecker-approved answers: aggregate
faithfulness is necessary but not sufficient for
evidence-binding.
Setup. For each of six RAGBench datasets (Hot-
potQA, TechQA, PubMedQA, CovidQA, EMan-
ual, FinQA), we synthesize coherent update and
contradiction documents with Claude Haiku condi-
tioned on the question and the reference answer
(a 2–3 sentence passage asserting a specific al-
ternative answer of the same factual type; Ap-
pendix I), then run the standard COUNTERMEM
update- and contradiction-addition interventions.
A per-row audit by Claude-Sonnet finds 93.6%
of these coherence-conditioned contradiction pay-
loads well-formed (the 82% of Table 15 is a differ-
ent audit, of the one-line templated payloads in the
27-configuration sweep; Appendix J); we restrict
the headline subset to RAGChecker-approved an-
swers (rch_hall ≤ 0.10) with judged-valid contra-
dictions (n=446, out of n=593 generated). Each
baseline answer is scored by RAGChecker; we
measure update following on the update interven-
tion and conflict acknowledgment on the contradic-
