6 Conclusion
We introduce URGen, a training-free framework
that uses context uncertainty to adapt retrieval
depth and complement answer uncertainty in
guideline-grounded medical multiple-choice QA.
On TED with Llama-3.1-8B, URGen signiﬁcantly
reduces overconﬁdent errors against every ﬁxed
depth while maintaining accuracy comparable to
Fixed-1. Cross-backbone and sensitivity results
further support the method within curated disease-
scoped galleries. URGen’s uncertainty is a triage
signal rather than a correctness guarantee. Future
work should evaluate automatically extracted cor-
pora, additional specialties, and open-ended ques-
tions.
