Figure 3: Overview of the URGen pipeline for guideline-grounded medical question answering. (1) We parse
clinical guidelines and consensus documents into structured keypoint–passage pairs (k; t) for retrieval. Given a
query, a frozen LLM ranks all keypoints under multiple sampling temperatures, producing diverse orderings. (2)
Each ordering is converted into softmax-normalized relevance scores, which are aggregated into a uniﬁed relevance
distribution. We compute context uncertainty from this distribution and deﬁne its effective support. A broader dis-
tribution retrieves more passages, whereas a sharply peaked distribution retrieves fewer. (3) The selected passages
are concatenated with the query and passed to the frozen LLM under multiple answer-generation temperatures, pro-
ducing candidate answers. (4) We aggregate the predicted options and calculate answer uncertainty from their vote
distribution. The ﬁnal uncertainty fuses normalized context and answer uncertainty. Finally, the pipeline outputs a
ﬁnal prediction ˆa accompanied by a calibrated uncertainty estimate ˆu.
