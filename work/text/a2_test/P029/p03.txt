3 Open-Source RAG Trace: RAGPULSE
To bridge the gap between RAG systems re-
search and real-world deployment, we introduce
RAGPULSE, an open-source RAG workload trace
collected from a production university-wide policy
question-answering system. The service has been
continuously operating since April 2024 and serves
over 40,000 students and faculty members. We
release the core trace of RAGPULSE as a public
dataset and commit to its continuous update as the
system evolves.
