6 CONCLUSION
We propose RAG-EDA, a customized retrieval augmented genera-
tion (RAG) flow for electronic design automation (EDA) tool docu-
mentation question-answering (QA). We develop domain-specific
training strategies and use EDA-related corpus to customize the text
embedding, reranker and generator models for RAG-EDA. Besides,
we also develop and release ORD-QA, a documentation QA eval-
uation benchmark based on OpenROAD. RAG-EDA has achieved
much better performance on both ORD-QA and a commercial EDA
tool documentation QA benchmark compared with other state-of-
the-arts.
