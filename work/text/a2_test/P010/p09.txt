V. LIMITATIONS AND FUTURE WORK
This work aims to develop a generalisable explainable
driving agent using a Multi-Modal Large Language Model
(MLLM), addressing a significant obstacle that has hindered
deployment: the poor generalisation capacity. However, as an
early exploration, we still observe several limitations that need
to be addressed in future works to realize the application of
such promising technology.
