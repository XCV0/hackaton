fusion to mismatched vocabularies is an emerg-
ing direction; recent work has explored token-level
collaboration across models with different vocabu-
laries (Bian et al., 2025). Such approaches suggest
that an alignment layer, e.g., based on prefix group-
ing or token mapping, could enable auxiliary mod-
els with different tokenizers to provide compatible
guidance within GRAD. We leave this extension
to future work.
Finally, our main experiments focus on single-
turn RAG. We provide initial iterative/multi-hop
results using HQA in Appendix E to show that
single-turn gains carry over, but fully generalizing
the adaptation mechanism to multi-turn pipelines
remains open.
