these passes are independent given the same prefix,
so the wall-clock overhead can be further reduced
in a parallel implementation.
5 Analysis
Beyond the main results, we examine the mech-
anism and generality of GRAD, showing that
its gains arise from transferable guidance signals
rather than from a narrow choice of helper model,
backbone family, or deployment setting.
Guidance from weaker models Table 5 indeed
shows that while finetuned SLMs show improve-
ments over their base model, they still struggle to
outperform the larger base LLM (8B) on most tasks.
However, by leveraging their combined knowledge,
seemingly weak signals from these smaller models
can improve much stronger models, reaffirming the
effectiveness of GRAD, capturing the most signifi-
cant adaptation signals in logit space. Finally, each
