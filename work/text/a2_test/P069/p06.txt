4.2 Experimental Results
In this section, we aim to answer the following
research questions:
• (RQ1) Do decoding objectives implemented
with smaller models align with RAG strate-
gies?
• (RQ2) Does GRAD realize effective task-
adaptive combination of objectives?
• (RQ3) Does GRAD generalize to private,
domain-specific RAG scenarios?
• (RQ4) Does GRAD achieve a better cost-
performance trade-off?
