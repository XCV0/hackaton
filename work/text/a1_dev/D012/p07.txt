The chemical compositions of CZTS thin f ilms prepared using different DIO additive 
concentrations were investigated using EDS analysis. Figure 5 shows the chemical compo-
sitions of CZTS thin films prepared using the DIO additive concentration of 20 mg/mL. The 
EDX spectra shown in Figure 5 confirm the presence of all predictable constitutive elements 
of CZTS. For the sample with 20 mg/mL of DIO, the atomic percent ratio of Cu: Zn:Sn:S was 
24.2:13.3:12.3:50.2, which indicates that the material was copper-rich but close to the 
Cu:Zn:Sn:S = 2:1:1:4 ratio, confirming the formation of the stoichiometric Cu 2ZnSnS4 com-
position.

compositions of the CZTS active layers in Table 1 also have a chemical composition Cu-poor
and Zn-rich stoichiometry. The Cu/(Zn + Sn) ratio of the CZTS thin ﬁlms decreased as

Zn/Sn ratio was greater than 1, which is preferred to obtain the desired stoichiometric
composition for high photoconversion in photovoltaic applications [29,30].
