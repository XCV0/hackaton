9.   Long-term stability analysis
Type of analysis, bias conditions and environmental 
conditions
Yes
No
For shelf stability test, unencapsulated devices were aged in nitrogen atmosphere 
under dark conditions at 35 ± 5 degree Celsius for 6,000 hours. 
For operational stability test, the unencapsulated devices were aged under open-
circuit condition and continuous LED light illumination (100 mW/cm^2) in nitrogen 
atmosphere at 60 ± 5 degree Celsius for 1,500 hours.
