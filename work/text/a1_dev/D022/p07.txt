J–V (current density–voltage) measurements under illumination were
carried out using a AAA solar simulator with AM1.5G illumination cali-
brated using a NREL-certiﬁed Si reference solar cell (Abet Technologies
Model 15150). Electrical measurements were carried out with a source-
measure unit (Keithley SMU 2400) in four-wire sense mode, controlled
by the software Tracer (ReRa solutions) using a IEEE 488 GPIB
Instrument Control Device (National Instruments GPIB-USB-HS). For
these optoelectronic measurements, the sample was measured with a
mask and has been placed in a dark opaque matt in order to avoid any
back-reﬂections that could alter the characterization of the transparent
solar cells.
