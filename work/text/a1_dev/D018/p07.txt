solar cells, the JSC, VOC, FF, and ɳ were 31.3 mA/cm2, 0.410 V, 59%,
and 7.8%, respectively. Thus, our Cs-ST process increases the VOC by
/C2480 mV and improves the performance by more than 30%. This VOC
increment is similar to or even higher than that obtained for vacuum
deposition of CsF in Se atmosphere.29,30 We additionally cross-
checked the effect of the Cs-ST treatment using CIGS absorbers from
an industry-scale fabrication (NICE Solar Energy GmbH) and found a
similar improvement in VOC, and ɳ for the best performing solar cells
(see statistical analysis in Figure 6B–E).
4 | CONCLUSION
In summary, we presented an alternative to the vacuum-based
AlkF-PDT by a solution-based PDT using a CsCO3 solution (Cs-ST).
No major changes in the surface morphology of the Cs-ST CIGS thin
films were observed. Nevertheless, XPS measurements confirm the
presence of Cs at the surface region and a lowering of the VBM by
around 0.25 eV for the Cs-ST sample. HR-TEM indicates the presence
of Cs and depletion of Cu at the grain boundaries of the CIGS thin film
only up to a depth of a few hundred nanometers from the surface.
Unlike the vacuum Alk-PDT, our Cs-ST process prevents Cs from
reaching the CIGS/Mo interface, which prevents the related lowering
of the VBM at the CIGS/Mo interface.
