Generally, in high-efficiency CIGS solar cells, two-dimensional
(2D) monoclinic Alk-In-Se forms on top of the Cu-depleted
three-dimensional (3D) chalcopyrite structure.43,44 This 2D/3D mono-
clinic/chalcopyrite interface is believed to passivate dangling bonds.44
Similarly, we hypothesize that a 2D/3D interfacial structure of
Cs-O- /CIGS also forms in our Cs-ST CIGS thin films.
The change in the surface properties of the Cs-ST samples
(i.e., the presence of -O-Cs) was further confirmed by measuring
the surface energy via contact angle measurement. The presence of
-O-Cs groups at the surface changes the hydrophilic surface property
to hydrophobic.39 Indeed, the contact angle for the Cs-ST CIGS
thin film increased to 84/C14 from 76/C14 for the untreated CIGS sample
(Figure 3F,G).
Furthermore, it has been reported that -O-Cs termination at sur-
faces alters the work function of metal electrodes38,39 and the valence
band maximum (VBM) of semiconductors.32–36 Therefore, UPS mea-
surements were carried out on the CIGS thin films with and without
Cs-ST (Figure 4). The VB edge positions are determined at (0.65 ± 0.1)
eV and (0.40 ± 0.1) eV with respect to the Fermi level position, for the
Cs-ST and untreated CIGS thin films, respectively (upper left inset in
Figure 4). Thus, the VBM of the CIGS thin films lowers by around
0.25 eV upon the Cs-ST treatment, which is expected to reduce the
recombination at the buffer/CIGS interface and to improve the device
performance. For the AlkF-PDT evaporation in a vacuum with
different alkali elements, a much larger change in VBM from /C00.12 eV
to /C01.15 eV has been reported.45 In contrast, the treatment with KI
solution on CIGS thin films has recently been reported to lower the
VBM by /C00.15 eV,28 in good agreement with our CsCO3 Cs-ST
sample.
Furthermore, we performed Kelvin probe force microscopy
(KPFM) to get information about the work function change upon the
Cs-ST.46 Figure 4B shows the typical topography of CIGS thin films
with a polycrystalline morphology and grains of about 1 μm. The
corresponding contact potential difference (CPD) images show fairly
homogeneous grains with slightly higher CPD at the grain boundaries.
The CPD data indicates that the work function of the Cs-ST CIGS is
about 170 meV lower than that of the untreated CIGS, in agreement
with the expected lowering of the work function upon the -O-Cs
surface termination.
