Our results indicate that the Cs-ST modifies the CIGS thin film at
the surface by the formation of a -O-Cs surface termination and the
presence of Cs in the GBs mainly in the top half of the absorber layer
(see schematic illustration in Figure 5H). This contrasts with the
vacuum Alk-PDT process, where heavy alkali metals diffuse from the
surface to the CIGS/Mo interface. Their presence lowers the VBM at
the front and back regions of the CIGS thin film.47,48 The lower VBM
at the CIGS/Mo back contact interface can generate a secondary
diode during the device operation.47 However, our Cs-ST process
modifies the CIGS thin film only in the near-surface region, thereby
preventing the formation of a secondary diode at the CIGS/Mo
interface.
