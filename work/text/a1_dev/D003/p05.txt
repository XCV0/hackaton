giveninTableS2.Accordingtothebasictheoryforband
arrangement of heterojunctions, the band bending at
the interface leads to an energy barrier at the interface,
followed by suffering charge accumulation and energy
loss.53 The conduction band minimum (CBM) of both
Sn3O4 and Y-Sn3O4 is close enough to the CBM of perov-
skite so that charge accumulation at the interface could
be relieved.53 In addition to the position of the conduc-
tion band, the position of the Fermi energy level (EF)
also has an effect on the device. The contact interface
between ETL and perovskite will undergo energy band
bending and the EF will be flattened. In order to keep
the EF flat at the interface, the energy level bending of
perovskite will increase when the EF of ETL increases
caused by doping. Under open circuit and illumination
conditions, the electron quasi-Fermi energy level (EF,n)
will upshifted if the EF of ETL increases. So, quasi-Fermi
energy level splitting (QFLS), which is the difference
between the EF,n and hole quasi-Fermi energy level
(EF,h), also increases. The value of VOC in the photovol-
taic device is determined by the QFLS. This is
influenced by the occupancy of available electronic
states by photogenerated charge carriers, not only in the
perovskite layer, but also the charge transport layer.
Therefore, EF upshift of ETL can also increase the QFLS
and expand the VOC.54,55
