Figure 3B,C shows the results of the current mode
AFM images.46 The Y-Sn3O4 film has a higher average
current at the same voltage, implying a better conductivity,
in agreement with the J-V test. The average current values
on the film surface are 0.5 and 1.8 nA at bias voltage of
1 V, respectively. Figure 3D shows the J–V plot for analyz-
ing the electronic conductivity. The conductivity σ is calcu-
lated based on the J–V diagram. The thickness of the
Sn3O4,1%Y-Sn3O4,5%Y-Sn3O4 and 10% Y-Sn3O4 sam-
ples was obtained by scanning electron micrographs
(SEM) measurements of 114, 102, 99, and 95 nm. The σ
of Sn3O4,1%Y-Sn3O4,5%Y-Sn3O4 and 10% Y-Sn3O4
is 6.6 /C2 10/C04,1.52/C2 10/C03,2.05/C2 10/C03 and 3.25 /C2
10/C03 Sm/C01. The sample structure is ITO/Sn3O4/Ag.
When the doping is 10%, the J–V curve is no longer
straight, although the σ is also increased, probably due
tothecapacitiveeffectcausedbytheheterogeneous
phase. The conductivity depends on the electron concen-
tration at constant mobility. A suitable amount of Y dop-
ing increased the electron concentration and did not
introduce severe carrier scattering, thus increasing the
conductivity by a corresponding factor.
