3. Materials and Methods 
3.1. Device Fabrication 
Deposition of SnO2 films: All devices were deposited on clean fluorine-doped tin ox-
ide (FTO) glass. FTO glass was cleaned thoroughly in deionized water, ethyl alcohol, and 
isopropanol with the assistance of sonication. Prior to electron transport layer deposition, 
the substrates were subjected to UV/ozone treatment for 20 min at room temperature. 
SnO2 blocking layers were spin-coated onto FTO glass at 3000 rpm for 30 s, using SnO 2 
colloidal dispersion. All processes were conducted in an ambient air atmosphere.
