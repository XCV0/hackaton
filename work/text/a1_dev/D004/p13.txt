These combinations cooperatively worked to synthesize high-quality MoS2 NPs, which
facilitate the pin-hole free, large crystal growth of perovskite layers as supported by the
light absorption and crystallinity and morphological analysis by using UV, PL, XRD and
SEM. Therefore, such crystal growths enhanced the PCE significantly by lowering the
crystal defect density and trap sites existing in the thin films, improving the efficient charge
transport and thereby reducing charge recombination.
